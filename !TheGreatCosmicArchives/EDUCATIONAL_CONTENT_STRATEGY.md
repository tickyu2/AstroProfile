## ✍️ **Writing Standards**

### **Tone:**
```
AUTHORITATIVE but ACCESSIBLE
├─ Not: Academic jargon that alienates
├─ Not: Dumbed down that insults intelligence
└─ But: Clear, engaging, rigorous

EXAMPLE:
Bad: "The rabbit's Yin nature derives from its nocturnal, burrowing proclivities."
Good: "The Rabbit is Yin because it's nocturnal (active at night = Yin time), 
      gentle (receptive rather than aggressive), and lives underground 
      (Earth element, Yin direction)."
```

### **Length:**
```
SALAD (Expandable per factor): 200-400 words
MAIN COURSE (Master Class): 2000-5000 words
DESSERT (Sources): 10-20 citations
```

### **Structure:**
```
Every piece must have:
✓ Clear header hierarchy (H2, H3, H4)
✓ Visual icons for sections
✓ Cross-references to other content
✓ Sources/citations
✓ User's specific application
```

### **Citations:**
```
FORMAT:
• Book: Author, Title (Year)
• Paper: Author, "Title," Journal Vol.Issue (Year): pages
• Website: Organization, "Title," URL (accessed date)

EXAMPLE:
• Wilhelm, Richard. The I Ching or Book of Changes (1950)
• Kaptchuk, Ted. "The Web That Has No Weaver," Contemporary Books (2000)
• Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Yin and Yang," 
  plato.stanford.edu/entries/yin-yang (accessed Nov 2025)
```

## 🎓 **Authority Building Strategy**

### **What Makes Content Authoritative:**

1. **PRIMARY SOURCES**
   - Quote from I Ching, Huangdi Neijing, classical texts
   - Show we've read the originals (or good translations)

2. **CROSS-CULTURAL VERIFICATION**
   - "Chinese tradition says X. Indian tradition independently says Y. 
      Western tradition says Z. All three converge on..."
   - Synthesis = mastery

3. **SCIENTIFIC CORRELATION**
   - "Modern neuroscience confirms ancient Yin/Yang theory..."
   - Bridge ancient wisdom with contemporary research

4. **HONEST LIMITATIONS**
   - "We don't know exactly when..." (humble)
   - "Scholars debate whether..." (nuanced)
   - Trust through transparency

5. **VISUAL EXCELLENCE**
   - Beautiful diagrams
   - Clear hierarchy
   - Professional presentation
