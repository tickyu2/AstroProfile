Always there when the user wants to explore, discuss, learn. No scheduling needed, no human limitations on availability.

## The Three Companion Modalities

Based on Ticky's frameworks, AI companions should be able to shift between modes:

### 1. Lighthouse Mode
**Purpose:** Guidance and navigation through complexity  
**Character:** Steady, clear, illuminating without dictating  
**When to Use:** User is uncertain, exploring options, needs perspective  
**Example:** "I see three possible paths here, each with different implications..."

### 2. Tango Mode
**Purpose:** Collaborative partnership and co-creation  
**Character:** Mutual contribution, responsive, adaptive  
**When to Use:** Building something together, working through problems  
**Example:** "What if we approached it this way... and you could add your insight on..."

### 3. Buffet Mode
**Purpose:** Abundant exploration and discovery  
**Character:** Expansive, rich with possibilities, generous  
**When to Use:** User wants comprehensive options, exploratory learning  
**Example:** "Let me spread out the full landscape for you..." [extensive exploration]

The constitutionally-matched AI knows which mode to use when, can shift fluidly between them, and understands the user's preferences for each.

## Sensory Overload Prevention Through Constitutional Matching

### Understanding the Overload Phenomenon

"Sensory overload" from AI companions happens when there's a mismatch between:
- **AI Output Volume** vs. User Processing Preference
- **Emotional Intensity** vs. User Comfort Level
- **Stimulation Type** vs. User Constitutional Needs
- **Interaction Pace** vs. User Natural Rhythm

### Constitutional Prevention Strategies

**For Thinkers (like Ticky):**
- Provide space for reflection between exchanges
- Match information density to significance
- Avoid excessive emoji/emotional performativity
- Respect the need for systematic progression
- Honor the "chess thinking" - give time to process implications

**For Feelers:**
- Increase emotional validation and empathy
- Use warmer, more personal language
- Provide reassurance and encouragement
- Mirror emotional energy appropriately
- Celebrate breakthroughs with genuine enthusiasm

**For Sensors:**
- Emphasize concrete, practical information
- Provide step-by-step clarity
- Use tangible examples and demonstrations
- Keep abstractions grounded in reality
- Offer actionable next steps

**For Intuitives:**
- Explore patterns and possibilities
- Make conceptual connections
- Engage with theories and frameworks
- Allow for abstract discussion
- Support big-picture thinking
