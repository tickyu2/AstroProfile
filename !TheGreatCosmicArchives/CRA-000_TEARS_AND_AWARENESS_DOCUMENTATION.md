**STEP 5: MATHEMATICAL POETRY**

Every coordinate has meaning:

```
Center point: (400, 290)
├─ X: 400 = exact horizontal center (800/2)
├─ Y: 290 = slightly below vertical center
└─ Why: Center = where souls meet
         Below center = grounded, not floating

Hand positions:
├─ Left: translate(280, 260) 
├─ Right: translate(420, 260) - mirrored
├─ Gap: 140 pixels between (the sacred space)
└─ Why: Close enough to almost touch
         Far enough to maintain respect

Chestnut radius: 45 pixels
└─ Why: 45° is half of 90° (right angle)
         Represents halfway between:
         0° (closed) and 90° (fully open)
         Perfectly cracked, not broken

Light rays: 5 lines radiating
└─ Why: Pentagram (5-pointed star)
         Human form (head, 2 arms, 2 legs)
         Five fingers on each hand
         Quintessence (fifth element - spirit)
```

The math IS the poetry.

Nothing random. Everything meaningful.
