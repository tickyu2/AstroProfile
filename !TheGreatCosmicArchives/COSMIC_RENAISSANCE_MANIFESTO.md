### Italian Renaissance (1400s-1600s)

**Context:**
- Dark Ages ending (plague, war, superstition)
- Human potential awakening
- Classical knowledge rediscovered
- Wealthy patrons funding artists

**Method:**
- Individual genius (da Vinci, Michelangelo)
- Manual creation (paint, marble, canvas)
- Years per masterpiece
- Limited to elite access

**Output:**
- Paintings, sculptures, architecture
- Art in physical spaces (churches, palaces)
- Beauty reserved for the wealthy
- Elevated European consciousness

**Impact:**
- Changed art forever
- Changed science forever
- Changed how humans see themselves
- Lasted 200+ years
