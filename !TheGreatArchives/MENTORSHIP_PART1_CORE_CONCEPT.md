## 🎯 THE CORE IDEA IN 5 MINUTES

### **The Problem:**
- Mentorship is rare, informal, and uncompensated
- Gratitude is expressed but not sustained
- Success depends on luck and privilege
- Great mentors can't scale their impact

### **The GENESIS Solution:**

**THE 10% LIFETIME MENTORSHIP BOND**

```
When someone helps you succeed:
├─ You pay them 10% of your income INCREASE
├─ Not 10% of total income
├─ But 10% of (Current Income - Expected Baseline)
├─ For lifetime (or until reasonable cap)
└─ Everyone wins proportionally
```

---

## 💡 HOW IT WORKS

### **Step 1: Establish Baseline**
```
Your baseline = Expected income without mentorship
Based on:
├─ Education level
├─ Location
├─ Years experience
├─ Industry
└─ Statistical models

Example: College grad in Austin, TX = $45,000 baseline
```

### **Step 2: Track Growth**
```
Year 1: You earn $60,000
├─ Baseline: $45,000
├─ Increase: $15,000
└─ Mentor receives: $1,500 (10%)

Year 5: You earn $120,000
├─ Baseline: $47,000 (adjusted for inflation)
├─ Increase: $73,000
└─ Mentor receives: $7,300 (10%)

Year 10: You earn $250,000
├─ Baseline: $50,000
├─ Increase: $200,000
└─ Mentor receives: $20,000 (10%)
```

### **Step 3: Lifetime Partnership**
```
Payments continue until:
├─ Cap reached (typically 10× original mentor investment)
├─ OR lifetime of both parties
├─ BUT relationship continues forever
└─ Gratitude becomes mathematical AND emotional
```
