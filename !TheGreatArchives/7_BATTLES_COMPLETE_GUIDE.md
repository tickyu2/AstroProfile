## ⚔️ THE 7 CONSTITUTIONAL BATTLES

Every person faces **7 Constitutional Battles** at birth. Each battle is won by Yin (receptive), Yang (active), or ends in Balance (adaptable).

###  **THE ULTIMATE BATTLE (20 points)**

**⚔️ Birth Time** - When Did Your Soul Arrive?

**Why 20 points (highest weight)?**
- Based on **Zi Wu Liu Zhu (子午流注)** - the "Noon-Midnight Ebb-Flow" doctrine
- In Chinese medicine, birth time determines which organ meridian receives dominant energy flow
- This is THE primary constitutional factor that practitioners have weighted most heavily for 3,000 years
- **Night births (6 PM - 6 AM):** Kidney, Liver, Lung meridians dominant → Yin energy
- **Day births (6 AM - 6 PM):** Heart, Stomach, Intestine meridians dominant → Yang energy  
- **Transition times (Dawn 5-7 AM, Dusk 5-7 PM):** Adaptable, shifting energy → Balanced

**This is not arbitrary.** Ancient texts teach that your birth hour determines your life energy flow pattern.

#### **⚔️ Battle 1: Chinese Animal**
Your lunar year animal represents core temperament and social mask.

**Why 15 points?**
- Foundational personality element

- Modified by other factors but forms the base

**Examples:**
- Rabbit (Yin) - Diplomatic, gentle, peace-seeking
- Dragon (Yang) - Charismatic, ambitious, commanding

- Ancient healing systems use this as diagnostic foundation

- Fire (Yang) - Transformative, passionate, rising

### **THE MINI BATTLES (10 points each)**

#### **⚔️ Battle 5: Birth Day / Planetary Ruler**
Each weekday ruled by a planet with distinct energy.

**Why 10 points (mini battle)?**
- Supportive influence, not primary determinant

**Examples:**
- Wednesday/Mercury (Balanced) - Communication, versatility
- Tuesday/Mars (Yang) - Action, aggression, drive
- Monday/Moon (Yin) - Reflection, emotion, intuition

- Biology whispers; other factors shout

## 🚨 TROUBLESHOOTING

### **Problem: Files won't copy**
```powershell
# Check you're in the right directory
pwd
# Should show: C:\Users\YourName\AstroProfile

# Check Downloads folder location
dir ~\Downloads\calculations_7_BATTLES.js

```

### **Problem: Dev server won't start**
```powershell
# Clear node modules and reinstall
Remove-Item -Recurse -Force node_modules
npm install
npm run dev
```

### **Problem: Changes not showing**
```powershell
# Hard refresh browser
# Press: Ctrl + Shift + R

# Or clear browser cache
# Press: Ctrl + Shift + Delete
```

### **Problem: Console errors**
1. Open browser console (F12)
2. Look for red errors
3. Check if import paths are correct in files
4. Verify both files were copied correctly
