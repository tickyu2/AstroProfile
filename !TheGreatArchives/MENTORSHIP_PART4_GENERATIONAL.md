## 🌳 THE GENERATIONAL WEALTH MODEL

### **What We Mean by "Generational Wealth":**

**OLD DEFINITION (Money Only):**
```
Parents make money
  ↓
Leave inheritance to children
  ↓
Children spend it down
  ↓
By 3rd generation: Gone

Result: "Shirtsleeves to shirtsleeves in 3 generations"
```

**NEW DEFINITION (Wisdom + Money):**
```
Parents gain WISDOM (through mentorship)
  ↓
Apply wisdom → Make money + Document learnings
  ↓
TEACH wisdom to children (inheritance of knowledge)
  ↓
Children apply SAME wisdom → Make MORE money
  ↓
Children teach grandchildren (compounding wisdom)
  ↓
Each generation STRONGER than last

Result: Exponential growth across generations
```
