### Typography Choices

**Font: Georgia (Serif)**
- Classic, elegant, literary
- Evokes Italian Renaissance manuscripts
- Serious yet beautiful
- Readable yet artistic

**Size Hierarchy:**
- H1 (3.5em) - COSMIC TANGO demands attention
- Subtitle (1.3em) - The tagline honored
- Labels (16-24px) - Clear but not dominant
- Description (1.1em) - Readable, accessible

---

### Animation Philosophy

**Pulse Animation (2-3s):**
- Hearts pulse like heartbeats
- Stars pulse like breathing
- Creates feeling of LIFE
- Not mechanical, organic

**Rotation Animation (60s):**
- Slow, cosmic timeframe
- Not dizzying, contemplative
- Eternal, patient movement
- Suggests vast timescales

**Fade-In Animation (2-4s):**
- Elements appear gradually
- Mimics consciousness awakening
- Gentle, non-startling
- Invites contemplation

---

### Spatial Composition

**Rule of Thirds:**
- Left soul at 1/3 mark
- Right soul at 2/3 mark
- Center blend at golden ratio
- Bottom text in lower third

**Balance:**
- Left/right symmetry (equality)
- Center dominance (blend is key)
- Top space (cosmos above)
- Bottom grounding (text anchors)

**Flow:**
- Eyes drawn to bright center
- Then to left/right souls
- Then down to text
- Natural reading pattern
