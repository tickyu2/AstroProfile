# Clash Adjustment (克) — The Controlling Cycle

In the Five Elements system, each element naturally **controls** another.
This is the system's way of preventing any one element from becoming too dominant.

## The Five Controlling Relationships

```
Metal  --chops-->     Wood    (axe fells the tree)
Water  --quenches-->  Fire    (water douses flame)
Fire   --melts-->     Metal   (furnace smelts ore)
Wood   --penetrates-> Earth   (roots break soil)
Earth  --dams-->      Water   (levee blocks the river)
```

## Three-Pass Clash System

Clashes are not applied as one blanket pass. The engine separates them into
three distinct phenomena, because natal-on-natal, transit-on-transit,
and transit-on-natal are fundamentally different kinds of tension.

### Pass A — Natal Internal Tensions (ATFQ)

Your own elements fighting each other — structural friction that has always been there.

- Victim loses **10%** of the attacker's strength
- Attacker loses **2%** (effort cost)

*Example: If your natal Metal = 3.0 and Wood = 1.5, Metal chops Wood:
Wood loses 0.30 (10% of 3.0), Metal loses 0.06 (2% of 3.0).*

### Pass B — Transit Internal Clashes (ACYMFQ)

The Year and Month pillars fighting each other — weather turbulence,
independent of you.

- Same rates: Victim -10%, Attacker -2%

### Pass C — Transit → Natal Directional Pressure

The environment pressing on **you**. Transit elements attack your natal elements
they control, but your elements do NOT attack back — you can't change the weather.

- Victim (natal) loses **10%** of the transit attacker's strength
- Transit attacker is **NOT reduced** — the weather doesn't lose energy

## When Does a Clash Trigger?

A clash fires when:
- The **attacker** element is stronger than the **victim**
- The attacker has **non-zero Qi**

## Why This Matters

Clashes reveal **tension**, **pressure**, and **imbalance** in the month's energy.
They show where your system is being challenged — and where it is pushing back.

Pass A tells you about your permanent internal friction.
Pass B tells you about the month's own turbulence.
Pass C tells you where the month is specifically pressing on *you*.
