# Transformation (化) — Intermediate

Transformation is the most dramatic adjustment in the pipeline.
It only fires under extreme imbalance — when one element completely overwhelms another.

## Trigger Conditions (Both Required)

| Condition | Threshold | Why |
|-----------|-----------|-----|
| Attacker Qi | > 1.5 points | Must have real substance, not a rounding artifact |
| Attacker / Victim ratio | > 3:1 | Must be genuinely overwhelming |

A 2:1 ratio is a clash. A 3:1+ ratio with force behind it is a metamorphosis.

## Transformation Products

The victim doesn't vanish — 30% of it transmutes into its **productive-cycle child**:

| Attacker | Victim | Product | Description |
|----------|--------|---------|-------------|
| Fire | Metal | Water | Fire melts Metal into liquid |
| Metal | Wood | Fire | Metal strikes Wood, sparks fly |
| Water | Fire | Earth | Water drowns Fire, leaves sediment |
| Wood | Earth | Metal | Wood uproots Earth, exposes ore |
| Earth | Water | Wood | Earth absorbs Water, enables growth |

## Effect

- **30%** of the victim's Qi converts to the child element
- The victim keeps the remaining **70%**, weakened but present
- The attacker is unchanged

## Why Last in Pipeline?

Transformation is crisis-level alchemy. If clashes (克), nourishment (生),
and damping (耗) have already resolved the imbalance, transformation doesn't
fire. It only triggers when extreme pressure persists after everything else.
This prevents false triggers from transient spikes.
