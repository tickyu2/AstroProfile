# Clash Adjustment (克) — Intermediate

Clashes happen when one element becomes stronger than the element it controls
in the Five Element controlling cycle.

## The Five Controlling Pairs

| Attacker | Victim | Image |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Metal | Wood | Axe chops tree |
| Water | Fire | Water douses flame |
| Fire | Metal | Furnace smelts ore |
| Wood | Earth | Roots break soil |
| Earth | Water | Levee blocks river |

## When Does a Clash Fire?

Two conditions:
- The attacker must be **stronger** than the victim
- The attacker must have **non-zero** Qi

## Effects

- Victim loses **10%** of the attacker's Qi value
- Attacker loses **2%** of its own value (effort cost)

Example: If Metal = 4.0 and Wood = 2.0, Metal controls Wood.
- Wood loses 0.40 (10% of 4.0)
- Metal loses 0.08 (2% of 4.0)

## Three-Pass System

The engine separates clashes into three distinct passes:

| Pass | Pool | What It Shows |
|------|------|--------------|
| **A** | Natal (60%) | Your permanent internal tensions |
| **B** | Transit (40%) | The month's own turbulence |
| **C** | Transit -> Natal | The environment pressing on you |

In Pass C, the transit attacker is **not reduced** — you can't weaken the weather.
The natal victim absorbs the full pressure.

After all three passes, the results are recombined:
Post-Clash = Pass C natal result + Pass B transit result.
