# Yong Shen (用神) — Intermediate Guide

Your Yong Shen is the element your chart needs most this month.
Think of it as a prescription that changes with the seasons.

---

## The Basics

After all Qi adjustments run (clashes, combinations, sheng, etc.),
the engine looks at the final element distribution and asks:

**"What does this chart need right now?"**

---

## Three Possible Answers

### 1. Balanced (Green)

All five elements are within a healthy range (roughly 10-30% each).
Small deficits are filled with gentle, polarity-matched stones.

No heavy intervention needed. Maintenance mode.

### 2. Critical Imbalance (Red)

One element has grown above 40%. This is a threat.

The engine prescribes:
- The **controller** of the threat (e.g., Water to control Fire)
- The **exhaustor** of the threat (e.g., Earth to drain Fire)

And forbids:
- The threat element itself
- The **mother** of the threat (e.g., Wood — because Wood feeds Fire)

### 3. Collapse Override (Purple)

The chart has collapsed into an extreme structure. Normal rules
no longer apply. The engine switches to classical special-structure
rules:

- **Follow the Strong**: One element dominates so completely that
  you flow WITH it instead of fighting it
- **Bridge the Poles**: Two elements dominate — use a mediator
- **Feed the Drained**: One element nearly gone — nourish through
  its parent, not directly
- **Counter the Inverted**: Lopsided ratio — apply direct control

---

## Why It Changes Monthly

Your natal chart is fixed, but the Year and Month pillars change.
Each month brings different elemental weather. A month of strong
Fire transit can turn a balanced chart into a Fire-dominant one.

That is why the engine recalculates Yong Shen for every month —
your stone recommendations may shift with the seasons.

---

## Reading the Yong Shen Panel

At the bottom of each month's expanded view, you will see:

- **Status**: Balanced / Critical / Collapse Override
- **Reasoning**: Plain-language explanation of why
- **Forbidden**: Elements to avoid (with explanation)
- **Stones**: Specific crystal recommendations ranked by priority

---

## Connection to Stones

The Yong Shen decision directly controls which stones appear in
your bracelet prescription:

1. Yong Shen identifies useful elements
2. Polarity filter matches your Day Master (Yang/Yin)
3. Stone database picks the best crystals for those elements
4. Each stone gets a priority score and classical metaphor

The higher the priority, the more urgently that stone is needed.
