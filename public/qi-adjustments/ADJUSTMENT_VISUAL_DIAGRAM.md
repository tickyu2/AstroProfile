## The Five Cycles at Work

```
         Wood
        /    \
  (生)↗      ↘(克)
     /        \
  Water ←(克)── Fire
    ↑  \      / ↑
 (生)|   \  /   |(生)
    |    /\    |
  Metal←────Earth
      (克)
```

**Generating (生) clockwise:** Water → Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water
**Controlling (克) star pattern:** Wood → Earth → Water → Fire → Metal → Wood
