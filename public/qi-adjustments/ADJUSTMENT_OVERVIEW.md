# Adjustment Overview — How Your Monthly Qi Is Refined

Your Month Final Functional Qi (MFFQ) is created through a sequence
of elemental adjustments. Each step models a different kind of
energetic interaction — from tension, to support, to natural friction,
to rare alchemical transformation.

Below is the full journey your Qi takes each month.

## Step 6: Clash Adjustment (克) — Tension & Suppression

Every element has a natural control target:
Metal chops Wood, Water quenches Fire, Fire melts Metal,
Wood penetrates Earth, Earth dams Water.

The engine runs **three separate clash passes**:

| Pass | What clashes | Meaning |
|------|-------------|---------|
| A | Natal vs natal | Your permanent internal tensions |
| B | Transit vs transit | The month's own weather turbulence |
| C | Transit → natal | The environment pressing on you |

In Passes A & B, victim loses 10% of attacker, attacker loses 2%.
In Pass C, victim (you) loses 10% of transit attacker — but the
transit attacker is not reduced (you can't weaken the weather).

---

## Step 7: Sheng Cycle Nourishment (生) — Support & Growth

The Generating Cycle shows how elements feed one another:
Wood -> Fire -> Earth -> Metal -> Water -> Wood.

When a parent element is stronger than its child, the child
receives a **small boost** (3% of parent, capped at 20% of child).

The parent is not drained — this is gentle feeding, not sacrifice.

---

## Step 8: Universal Damping (耗) — Environmental Friction

All elements experience a small energetic cost just for existing
in a dynamic system.

Every element is reduced by **2%** (multiplied by 0.98).

This keeps the system grounded and prevents artificial inflation.

---

## Step 9: Transformation (化) — Extreme Alchemy

When one element overwhelms another (attacker > 1.5 pts AND
ratio > 3:1), the weaker element **changes form** into the
attacker's child element.

- 30% of the victim converts to the child element
- The victim shrinks; the child grows
- The attacker is unchanged

Transformation is rare, dramatic, and always meaningful — it marks
a moment of forced adaptation in the energetic landscape.

---

## Final Output: MFFQ

After all adjustments, you receive your **Month Final Functional Qi** —
the elemental landscape you will be traveling through this month.

This drives:
- **Yong Shen (用神)** — the "useful god" element you need for balance
- **Stone/crystal prescriptions** — specific stones mapped to weak elements
- **Awareness** — knowing where you'll feel pressure vs support
