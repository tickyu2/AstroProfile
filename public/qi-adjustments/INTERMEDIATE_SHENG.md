# Sheng Nourishment (生) — Intermediate

The Sheng (generating) cycle describes how elements naturally support each other.
After destructive clashes settle, the productive cycle gently rebuilds.

## The Five Generating Pairs

| Parent | Child | Image |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Water | Wood | Rain feeds the forest |
| Wood | Fire | Timber fuels the flame |
| Fire | Earth | Ash becomes soil |
| Earth | Metal | Mountains yield ore |
| Metal | Water | Minerals enrich springs |

## When Does Nourishment Apply?

- The **parent** is stronger than its **child**
- Both have positive Qi

If the child is already stronger, the parent has nothing to offer — the child
is thriving on its own.

## How It Works

| Step | Formula |
|------|---------|
| Raw boost | Parent Qi x 3% |
| Maximum cap | Child Qi x 20% |
| Actual boost | whichever is smaller |
| Parent effect | NOT reduced |

The cap prevents runaway inflation — a massive parent can't bloat a tiny child
beyond reason. And the parent is never drained; this is gentle feeding.

## Why After Clashes?

The storm passes first (克), then the sun comes out and the garden
starts recovering (生). Rebuilding on post-clash values ensures
accuracy — you can't nourish what hasn't survived the clash yet.
