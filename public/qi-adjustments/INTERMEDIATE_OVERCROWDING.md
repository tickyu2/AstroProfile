# Overcrowding (溢) — Intermediate

When one element becomes too dominant — holding more than 35% of total Qi
or exceeding 2x the five-element average — excess energy softly overflows
into its generating-cycle child.

## Trigger Conditions (Either One)

| Condition | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Share of total Qi | > 35% |
| Ratio to average | > 2.0x |

## Effect

- Excess = Element Qi minus (Average × 2.0)
- Bleed = 10% of excess, capped at 0.50 pts
- Parent element loses the bleed amount
- Child element gains the bleed amount

## Generating Cycle (Bleed Targets)

| Overcrowded | Bleeds Into |
|-------------|-------------|
| Wood | Fire |
| Fire | Earth |
| Earth | Metal |
| Metal | Water |
| Water | Wood |

## Pipeline Position

After Sheng, before Damping. This ensures Sheng-amplified dominance
is caught before universal friction and transformation fire.

## Key Distinction

Overcrowding is **gentle self-overflow** (10% of excess).
Transformation (化) is **dramatic external crushing** (30% of victim).
Both use the generating cycle, but for different reasons.
