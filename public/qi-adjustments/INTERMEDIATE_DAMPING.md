# Universal Damping (耗) — Intermediate

Even without clashes or nourishment, every element experiences natural friction
from existing in a dynamic Five Element system.

## The Rule

Every element is multiplied by **0.98** — a flat 2% reduction across the board.

No conditions. No exceptions. Applied to all five elements equally.

## What It Models

| Concept | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Energetic friction | No force operates without cost |
| Environmental resistance | The world pushes back on everything |
| Maintenance cost | Holding elemental balance requires energy |
| Natural entropy | Nothing stays at peak indefinitely |

## Why 2%?

The value is deliberately small — damping is background friction, not a dramatic
event. But applied to all five elements, it prevents the total Qi from
ballooning after Sheng nourishment adds energy back in.

## Pipeline Position

Damping comes third in the pipeline (after Clash and Sheng, before Transformation)
because it needs to operate on the most accurate post-nourishment values.
If it came earlier, Sheng would boost artificially deflated numbers.
