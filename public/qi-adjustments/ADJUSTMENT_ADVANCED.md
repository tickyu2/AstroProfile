# Monthly Qi Adjustment — For Advanced BaZi Students

This document assumes familiarity with Four Pillars of Destiny (四柱命理),
the Five Elements (五行), Heavenly Stems (天干), Earthly Branches (地支),
and Hidden Stems (藏干). It provides a rigorous technical explanation
of the monthly Qi adjustment pipeline.

---

## Philosophical Foundation

Classical BaZi treats the natal chart as fixed structure (命 Ming)
and annual/monthly cycles as dynamic influence (運 Yun).

This engine formalizes that distinction with a **60/40 normalization**:

```
ATFQ  = TFQ  x 0.60    (Adjusted natal — the Ming)
ACYMFQ = CYMFQ x 0.40   (Adjusted transit — the Yun)
```

The 60/40 ratio reflects the classical principle that the person's
structure is primary, but environmental Qi is non-trivial. The exact
ratio is a modeling choice; classical texts imply 60-70% natal
dominance in most frameworks.

## Pre-Pipeline: Functional Qi Computation

Each pillar (natal or transit) undergoes a five-section pipeline:

### A. Stem Element Attribution
The Heavenly Stem contributes **1 Qi point** to its element.
Yang stems carry full polarity; Yin stems carry inverted polarity.

### B. Branch Hidden Stem Distribution
The Earthly Branch contributes **10 Qi points** distributed across
its hidden stems (藏干) by classical percentage allocation.

Example: 寅 Tiger contains 甲 (60%), 丙 (30%), 戊 (10%).

### C. Birth Season Adjustment (令 Ling)
Each element is multiplied by its seasonal strength level:

| Level | Chinese | Multiplier | Classical Meaning |
|-------|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| 旺 Wang | Prosperous | x1.0 | Element rules the season |
| 相 Xiang | Phase | x0.8 | Generated by seasonal ruler |
| 休 Xiu | Resting | x0.6 | Generator of the ruler (spent) |
| 囚 Qiu | Imprisoned | x0.4 | Controlled by seasonal ruler |
| 死 Si | Dead | x0.2 | Controls the ruler but overwhelmed |

This follows the classical 五行旺相休囚死 framework.

**Critical distinction:** Natal pillars use birth month seasonality.
Transit pillars use the CURRENT month's seasonality.

### D. Polarity Adjustment (陰陽 Yin-Yang)
Based on Day Master polarity, elements receive slight amplification
or dampening. Yang charts amplify Wood (+15%) and Water (+10%);
Yin charts constrain them (-15%, -10%). Earth and Metal are neutral.

This models the classical observation that Yang Day Masters express
Wood and Water more forcefully, while Yin Day Masters internalize them.

### E. Qi Weighting (柱重 Pillar Weight)
Each pillar's contribution is scaled by its classical importance:

```
Year:       10%   (年柱 — ancestry, outer social environment)
Month:      30%   (月柱 — season, career, parents — the 令 commander)
Day Master: 35%   (日干 — the self, identity)
Day Branch: 15%   (日支 — spouse palace, internal reservoir)
Hour:       10%   (時柱 — children, inner mind, late life)
```

The Month pillar receives 30% because it carries 月令 (monthly command),
the single most important factor in classical strength assessment.

## The Three-Pass Clash System (三關克)

Classical BaZi distinguishes between internal chart tensions and
external cycle pressures. This engine formalizes that with three
separate clash passes:

### Pass A — Natal Internal Tensions (本命內克)

```
Input:  ATFQ (natal x 60%)
Apply:  Standard 克 cycle within natal pool only
Effect: Victim -= Attacker x 0.10; Attacker -= Attacker x 0.02
Output: ATFQ after internal resolution
```

**Classical parallel:** This corresponds to examining the natal chart
for 相克 relationships independent of 大運/流年. A chart with strong
Metal and weak Wood has permanent internal Wood suppression.

### Pass B — Transit Internal Clashes (運歲內克)

```
Input:  ACYMFQ (transit x 40%)
Apply:  Standard 克 cycle within transit pool only
Effect: Same rates as Pass A
Output: ACYMFQ after internal resolution
```

**Classical parallel:** The 流年 (annual pillar) and 流月 (monthly pillar)
can clash with each other. A Fire year meeting a Water month creates
transit-level turbulence independent of the native's chart.

### Pass C — Directional Transit Pressure (運克命)

```
Input:  Pass A result (natal) + Pass B result (transit)
Apply:  Transit elements attack natal elements they control
Effect: Natal victim -= Transit attacker x 0.10
        Transit attacker is NOT reduced
Output: Natal pool after environmental pressure
```

**Classical parallel:** This is the core 運克命 dynamic — the transit
cycle pressing on the natal chart. The transit element is not consumed
because 運 (luck/cycle) is environmental, not personal. You cannot
"use up" a season.

**Critically:** The natal pool does NOT attack back. Pass C is
**unidirectional**. This reflects the classical principle that the
person cannot alter the cosmic cycle — only respond to it.

### Recombination

```
Post-Clash NTFQ = Pass C natal result + Pass B transit result
```

The two pools merge back into a unified Qi landscape for subsequent
processing.

## Step 7: Sheng Cycle Nourishment (生)

After destructive 克 interactions resolve, the productive cycle
operates on the post-clash landscape:

```
Condition: Parent > Child AND Parent > 0
rawBoost  = Parent x 0.03
maxBoost  = Child x 0.20
boost     = MIN(rawBoost, maxBoost)
Child    += boost
Parent is NOT reduced
```

**Classical basis:** The 生 cycle is inherently supportive, not
extractive. Wood feeds Fire by its nature — it does not deplete
itself in doing so at this scale. The 3% rate and 20% cap prevent
the Sheng cycle from overwhelming the 克 adjustments that preceded it.

**Why after clashes:** In classical theory, 克 precedes 生 because
destructive forces settle first. The battlefield clears before
the garden grows.

---

## Step 8: Universal Damping (耗)

```
All elements x 0.98
```

This models 耗 (exhaustion/friction) — the universal cost of
maintaining elemental presence. In classical theory, no element
exists without 耗; even 旺 (prosperous) elements expend energy
to maintain dominance.

---

## Step 9: Transformation (化)

```
Condition: Attacker > 1.5 AND (Attacker / Victim) > 3.0
melt     = Victim x 0.30
Victim  -= melt
Product += melt
```

**Transformation products follow the productive cycle of the VICTIM:**

| 克 Pair | Victim | Victim's Child (Product) |
|---------|--------|--------------------------|
| Fire 克 Metal | Metal | Water (金生水) |
| Metal 克 Wood | Wood | Fire (木生火) |
| Water 克 Fire | Fire | Earth (火生土) |
| Wood 克 Earth | Earth | Metal (土生金) |
| Earth 克 Water | Water | Wood (水生木) |

**Classical parallel:** When 克 is extreme, the victim element
does not simply vanish — it undergoes 化 (transformation).
This reflects the principle that energy cannot be destroyed,
only transmuted. The victim's essence flows into its natural
productive child.

**Why last:** Transformation is crisis-level alchemy. It should
only trigger if, after all other adjustments (克 → 生 → 耗),
the imbalance persists. This prevents false triggers from
transient Qi spikes.

---

## Output: MFFQ and Yong Shen

The final MFFQ represents the person's functional elemental
landscape for the month. The Yong Shen (用神) is identified
as the element most needed for balance — classically, the
element that most benefits the Day Master's health and fortune.

Stone and crystal prescriptions map directly to the Yong Shen
and secondary supporting elements.

## Limitations and Future Work

This engine does **not** yet model:

- **He 合 combinations** (Heavenly Stem combinations, Earthly Branch combinations)
- **San He 三合** (Three Harmony combinations)
- **San Hui 三會** (Seasonal combinations)
- **Chong 沖** (Branch clashes / oppositions)
- **Xing 刑** (Branch punishments)
- **Hai 害** (Branch harm relationships)
- **Kong Wang 空亡** (Void/Emptiness)
- **Soft overcrowding** (element > 40% of total triggering overflow to child)

These represent v2.0 additions that would bring the engine closer
to a full classical BaZi strength assessment system.
