# Overcrowding — Self-Generated Instability (溢)

In classical BaZi, a chart with no clashes can still be deeply imbalanced.
When one element dominates the landscape, it creates **self-generated
instability** — the system buckles under its own weight.

This is the professional BaZi concept of **excess overflow**: when an
element is too strong, its excess energy naturally bleeds into its
generating-cycle child.

---

## When Does Overcrowding Trigger?

Two conditions are checked independently (either one triggers):

| Condition | Threshold | What It Means |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| Share of total | > 35% | This element holds more than a third of all Qi |
| Ratio to average | > 2.0x | This element is more than twice the five-element average |

Both are checked because a chart can have uneven totals. A 35% share
might not be 2x average in some configurations, and vice versa.

---

## What Happens?

When an element is overcrowded:

```
excess      = Element Qi - (Average × 2.0)
rawBleed    = excess × 10%
bleed       = MIN(rawBleed, 0.50)       // capped at 0.50 pts
Element     = Element - bleed            // parent loses
Child       = Child + bleed              // child gains
```

The bleed follows the **generating cycle** (生):

| Overcrowded | Bleeds Into |
|-------------|-------------|
| Wood | Fire |
| Fire | Earth |
| Earth | Metal |
| Metal | Water |
| Water | Wood |

---

## How It Differs From Transformation (化)

| | Overcrowding (溢) | Transformation (化) |
|---|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Share > 35% or ratio > 2x average | Attacker > 1.5 AND ratio > 3:1 |
| **Mechanism** | Excess overflows to generating child | Victim transmutes to controlling child |
| **Severity** | Gentle — 10% of excess, capped at 0.50 | Dramatic — 30% of victim |
| **Cause** | Self-dominance | External overwhelming |
| **Direction** | Parent → child (生 cycle) | Victim → victim's child |

Overcrowding is about **too much of a good thing**.
Transformation is about **being crushed by an opponent**.

---

## Why This Matters for Professional BaZi

Overcrowding explains:

- **Self-generated weakness** — extreme Wood doesn't make you strong in Wood;
  it makes you unstable because the excess overflows
- **Paradoxical imbalance** — a chart with no clashes can still need
  remediation if one element dominates
- **Advanced Yong Shen selection** — the "useful god" must account for
  overcrowding, not just deficiency
- **Structure collapse** — in classical theory, extreme dominance of one
  element can collapse the chart's structure

---

## Pipeline Position

```
克 Clash (3-pass) → 生 Sheng → 溢 Overcrowding → 耗 Damping → 化 Transform
```

Overcrowding sits between Sheng and Damping because:
1. Sheng may amplify a dominant element further (parent feeds child,
   but the child might already be dominant)
2. Overcrowding catches the post-Sheng peak
3. Damping then applies universal friction to the corrected values
4. Transformation fires last as crisis-level alchemy

## Configurable Thresholds

| Parameter | Default | Description |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Share threshold | 35% | Element must exceed this % of total Qi |
| Ratio threshold | 2.0× | Element must exceed this multiple of average |
| Bleed rate | 10% | Percentage of excess that bleeds to child |
| Max bleed | 0.50 pts | Hard cap on bleed amount per element |

These can be tuned for different BaZi schools or personal preferences.

---

## The Car Metaphor

Your engine is running too hot in one area. Rather than breaking down
(化 transformation), the excess heat **bleeds into the next system** —
the cooling system gets overworked, the electrical system heats up.

It's manageable. It's natural. But it's real — and ignoring it means
your monthly Qi assessment misses a key source of imbalance.
