# Combinations & Void — Intermediate

This guide explains how combinations and void modify your Qi
before the clash pipeline even begins.

---

## The 60/40 Blend Revisited

Your monthly Qi starts as NTFQ (60% natal + 40% transit).
Before ANY clashes, we check for combinations that can
transform this blend.

---

## Step 5a: Void (空亡)

Your Day Pillar determines two "void" branches.
Void branches provide ~12% less Qi and cannot fully
combine or clash.

Think of it as two cylinders running at reduced power.

---

## Step 5b: Combinations

Four types checked in order:

| Type | What | How Many | Effect |
|------|------|----------|--------|
| Stem Combo (天干合) | Pairs of 5 | 2 stems | Gentle transform |
| Six Combo (六合) | Pairs of 6 | 2 branches | Moderate transform |
| Three Harmony (三合) | Triads of 4 | 2-3 branches | Strong surge |
| Three Meetings (三会) | Seasonal triads | 3 branches | Massive dominance |

### Transform vs Bond
- **Transform**: Season supports the result → Qi actually shifts
- **Bond**: Season doesn't support → noted but no Qi change

### Clash Suppression (合能解冲)
Branches in active combinations are "protected" from clashes.
This is why some months that LOOK clashing feel peaceful.

---

## Pipeline Position

```
NTFQ → Void → Combos → Clash (3-pass) → Sheng → Overcrowding → Damping → Transform → MFFQ
```

---

## Example

Your chart has 寅 (Tiger) and 亥 (Pig) branches.
寅+亥 = Wood combination (六合).

If the current month is Spring (Wood season):
- Transform activates
- Wood Qi gets a significant boost
- Both branches protected from clashes

If current month is Autumn (Metal season):
- Bond only — no Qi change
- But branches still noted as "bonded" (clashes softened)
