# Universal Damping — Control Cycle Pressure (耗)

Even without clashes or nourishment, every element experiences **friction**
simply by existing in a dynamic system.

## The Rule

All elements undergo a small **2% reduction**:

```
element = element × 0.98
```

Every element. No exceptions. No conditions.

## Why Does This Happen?

This represents:

- **Energetic friction** — no force operates without cost
- **Environmental resistance** — the world pushes back on everything
- **Maintenance cost** — maintaining elemental balance requires energy
- **Natural entropy** — nothing stays at peak indefinitely

## An Analogy

Even a perfectly tuned car on a smooth highway burns fuel.
Even a healthy body at rest uses energy to breathe.

Damping is the baseline cost of being alive in a dynamic system.

## Why Damping Comes After Sheng

The order matters:

1. **Clashes (克)** — destructive tensions settle first
2. **Sheng (生)** — supportive nourishment rebuilds
3. **Damping (耗)** — universal friction applies to the result

If damping came before Sheng, the nourishment would operate on
artificially deflated values. The current order ensures the most
accurate modeling of how energy flows.

## Why This Matters

Damping prevents the system from becoming artificially inflated.
It keeps the month's Qi realistic, grounded, and dynamically alive.

The 2% is small by design — it is background friction, not a
dramatic event. But compounded across all five elements, it ensures
the total Qi naturally settles rather than ballooning.
