# Transformation (化) — Elemental Transmutation

Transformation is the **most dramatic** adjustment in the Five Elements system.

It occurs when one element **overwhelms** another so completely that the
weaker element **changes form** — transmuting into the child of the
controlling element.

## The Five Transformation Rules

| Attacker | Victim | Product | Description |
|----------|--------|---------|-------------|
| Fire | Metal | Water | Fire melts Metal into liquid (Water) |
| Metal | Wood | Fire | Metal strikes Wood, creating sparks (Fire) |
| Water | Fire | Earth | Water drowns Fire, leaving sediment (Earth) |
| Wood | Earth | Metal | Wood uproots Earth, exposing ore (Metal) |
| Earth | Water | Wood | Earth absorbs Water, enabling growth (Wood) |

## When Does Transformation Trigger?

Two conditions must **both** be met:

```
1. Attacker > 1.5 Qi points    (must be substantial)
2. Attacker / Victim > 3:1     (must be overwhelming)
```

Only **extreme imbalance** causes transformation.
A 2:1 ratio is a clash. A 3:1 ratio with real force behind it
is a metamorphosis.

## What Happens During Transformation?

```
melt    = Victim × 30%          (30% of victim transmutes)
Victim  = Victim − melt         (victim shrinks)
Product = Product + melt        (child element gains)
```

The victim does not disappear — only 30% changes form.
The remaining 70% persists, weakened but present.

## An Analogy

Imagine Fire (8.0 pts) overwhelming Metal (2.0 pts):
- Ratio = 4:1, and Fire > 1.5 — transformation triggers
- 30% of Metal (0.6 pts) transmutes into Water
- Metal drops from 2.0 to 1.4
- Water gains 0.6 pts

The intense heat literally melts the metal into liquid.

## Why Transformation Comes Last

Transformation is **crisis-level alchemy** — it only makes sense
after all other adjustments have settled:

1. Clashes reduce the dominant element (maybe below threshold)
2. Sheng may boost the weak element (maybe above threshold)
3. Damping applies universal friction

Only after all of that, if the ratio is **still** extreme,
does transformation fire. This prevents false triggers.

## Why This Matters

Transformation represents:

- **Overwhelm** — one force completely dominating another
- **Collapse of resistance** — the weaker element cannot hold its form
- **Forced adaptation** — energy changes shape to survive
- **Elemental alchemy** — destruction becomes creation

It is rare, powerful, and always meaningful. When you see
transformation in a month, pay attention — something fundamental
is shifting in the energetic landscape.
