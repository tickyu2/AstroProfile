# Adjustment Glossary

A quick reference for all elemental interactions and terms used
in the monthly Qi engine.

---

## Cycles

### Clash / Controlling Cycle (克 Ke)
One element suppresses another it naturally controls.
In internal passes: victim -10% of attacker, attacker -2%.
In directional pass: victim -10% of attacker, attacker unchanged.

### Sheng / Generating Cycle (生 Sheng)
Parent element nourishes its child.
Child gains up to 3% of parent's value, capped at 20% of child's value.
Parent is not reduced.

### Damping / Friction (耗 Hao)
Universal 2% reduction applied to all elements.
Models the baseline cost of existing in a dynamic system.

### Transformation / Transmutation (化 Hua)
Extreme imbalance (>1.5 pts AND >3:1 ratio) causes 30% of the
victim to convert into the controlling element's child.

---

## Roles

### Attacker
The element performing the controlling action in a clash.
Metal attacks Wood, Water attacks Fire, etc.

### Victim
The element being controlled or suppressed in a clash.

### Parent Element
The element that generates another in the Sheng cycle.
Wood is parent to Fire, Fire is parent to Earth, etc.

### Child Element
The element produced by its parent in the Sheng cycle.
Fire is child of Wood, Earth is child of Fire, etc.
