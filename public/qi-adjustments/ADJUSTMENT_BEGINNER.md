# Understanding Your Monthly Qi — For Absolute Beginners

No prior knowledge needed. Let's start from zero.

---

## What Is Qi?

Qi (pronounced "chee") is **energy** — the life force that flows
through everything. In Chinese philosophy, all energy is made up
of five elements:

| Element | What It Feels Like |
|---------|-------------------|
| Wood | Growth, ambition, creativity, spring energy |
| Fire | Passion, warmth, joy, summer energy |
| Earth | Stability, nurturing, grounding, harvest energy |
| Metal | Precision, clarity, structure, autumn energy |
| Water | Wisdom, flow, adaptability, winter energy |

Everyone has all five. But you have them in **different amounts**.

---

## What Is Your Natal Qi?

When you were born, the year, month, day, and hour created a unique
combination of elements. This is your **natal Qi** — think of it
as your elemental fingerprint.

Some people are born with lots of Fire and not much Water.
Some people have strong Metal but weak Wood.
Everyone is different.

**Your natal Qi never changes.** It is the foundation of who you are.

---

## What Changes Each Month?

The universe moves through cycles. Each month brings different
elemental energy — more Fire in summer, more Water in winter,
and so on.

Your **monthly Qi** is a blend:
- **60%** your natal Qi (the permanent you)
- **40%** the current month's energy (the temporary weather)

This blend is then adjusted through four natural processes.

---

## The Four Adjustments

### 1. Clash — When Elements Fight

Some elements naturally oppose each other:
- Water puts out Fire
- Metal chops Wood
- Fire melts Metal
- Wood breaks through Earth
- Earth blocks Water

When a strong element meets a weak one it controls,
the weak one loses some energy. This is called a **clash**.

**What it means for you:** Some months, parts of your energy
feel suppressed or challenged. That's a clash at work.

### 2. Nourishment — When Elements Help

Some elements naturally feed each other:
- Water helps Wood grow
- Wood fuels Fire
- Fire creates Earth (through ash)
- Earth produces Metal (from mountains)
- Metal enriches Water (minerals in springs)

When a strong "parent" element meets its weaker "child,"
the child gets a small energy boost.

**What it means for you:** Some months, certain energies feel
naturally supported and easy. That's nourishment at work.

### 3. Friction — The Cost of Living

Every element loses a tiny bit of energy (2%) each month,
just from existing in a dynamic world.

Think of it like your phone battery slowly draining even
when you're not using it. Everything costs a little energy.

### 4. Transformation — Rare, Big Shifts

Very rarely, one element is SO much stronger than another
(more than 3 times stronger) that the weaker element actually
**changes form**.

Think of ice melting into water — the substance changes shape
when the force is overwhelming.

This is rare. Most months it won't happen. When it does,
it's significant.

---

## What Comes Out?

After these four adjustments, you get your **Month Final Qi** —
a snapshot of your elemental energy for this specific month.

This tells us:
- Which elements are **strong** (you have plenty)
- Which elements are **weak** (you could use support)
- Which element is your **Yong Shen** (the one you need most)

## What Are the Stones For?

Each element maps to specific stones and crystals:

| Element | Example Stones |
|---------|---------------|
| Wood | Green Jade, Malachite, Aventurine |
| Fire | Red Jasper, Carnelian, Garnet |
| Earth | Tiger's Eye, Citrine, Yellow Jade |
| Metal | Clear Quartz, Moonstone, Hematite |
| Water | Lapis Lazuli, Aquamarine, Black Obsidian |

If your month shows weak Water, we recommend Water stones.
If Fire is overwhelming, we suggest Water or Earth stones
to bring balance.

**The bracelet doesn't change who you are.
It helps you navigate the month more smoothly.**
