# Monthly Qi Adjustments — Intermediate Level

This guide bridges the basics and the advanced classical theory.
It assumes you know the Five Elements and want to understand
how the pipeline modifies your monthly Qi.

---

## The 60/40 Blend

Your monthly Qi starts as a blend:
- **60%** from your natal chart (the permanent you)
- **40%** from the current year + month pillars (the weather)

This blend is called the **NTFQ** (Normalized Total Functional Qi).

---

## Step 6: Clash Adjustment (克)

The controlling cycle activates when one element overpowers another.

| Attacker | Victim | Effect |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Metal | Wood | Wood loses 10% of Metal's value |
| Water | Fire | Fire loses 10% of Water's value |
| Fire | Metal | Metal loses 10% of Fire's value |
| Wood | Earth | Earth loses 10% of Wood's value |
| Earth | Water | Water loses 10% of Earth's value |

The attacker also loses 2% (effort cost).

Clashes are computed in **three passes**:
- **Pass A** — natal elements fighting each other (internal tensions)
- **Pass B** — transit elements fighting each other (weather turbulence)
- **Pass C** — transit elements pressing on natal (weather hitting the car)

In Pass C, the transit attacker is not reduced — the weather doesn't lose energy.

---

## Step 7: Sheng Nourishment (生)

After clashes settle, the generating cycle kicks in. A strong parent
feeds its child:

- Boost = Parent x 3%, capped at Child x 20%
- The parent is NOT reduced

This is gentle rebuilding after the storm.

---

## Step 8: Universal Damping (耗)

All elements lose 2% (multiplied by 0.98).

This models natural friction — the cost of maintaining elemental presence.
It's small but universal, keeping the system grounded.

---

## Step 9: Transformation (化)

Under extreme conditions (attacker > 1.5 AND ratio > 3:1), the victim
partially transmutes into its productive-cycle child:

- 30% of the victim converts to the child element
- The victim keeps 70%, weakened but present
- The attacker is unchanged

Transformation only fires if the imbalance persists after all other
adjustments — it's the last resort.

---

## Step 10: MFFQ

The final output is your **Month Final Functional Qi** — the elemental
landscape you'll navigate this month.

This determines your **Yong Shen** (the element you need most) and
your **stone/crystal recommendations**.
