# Sheng Cycle Nourishment (生) — The Generating Cycle

Where the Controlling Cycle restrains, the **Generating Cycle (生)** nourishes.
Each element naturally **feeds** another — this is the cycle of growth,
support, and replenishment.

## The Five Generating Relationships

```
Water  --nourishes-->  Wood    (rain feeds the forest)
Wood   --feeds-->      Fire    (timber fuels the flame)
Fire   --creates-->    Earth   (ash becomes soil)
Earth  --bears-->      Metal   (mountains yield ore)
Metal  --enriches-->   Water   (minerals enrich springs)
```

## When Does Nourishment Happen?

Nourishment applies when:
- The **parent** element is stronger than its **child**
- Both elements have positive Qi

If the parent is weaker than the child, there is nothing to give — the child
is already thriving.

## How Nourishment Works

```
rawBoost = Parent × 3%           (3% of parent's strength)
maxBoost = Child × 20%           (capped at 20% of child's current value)
boost    = MIN(rawBoost, maxBoost)
Child    = Child + boost          (child is nourished)
Parent is NOT reduced             (gentle feeding, not exhaustion)
```

The cap prevents runaway growth — a strong parent cannot inflate a tiny child
beyond reason. And the parent is never drained; nourishment is gentle,
not sacrificial.

## Why Sheng Comes After Clashes

The generating cycle operates on the **post-clash landscape**.
First the destructive tensions settle, then the supportive relationships
gently rebuild what they can.

Think of it as: the storm passes (克), then the sun comes out and
the garden starts recovering (生).

## Why This Matters

The Sheng Cycle shows where your system is being **supported**, **fed**,
and **strengthened** this month. It highlights the natural flow of growth
in the month's energy — the quiet, constructive force that rebuilds
after tension.
