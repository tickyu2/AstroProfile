# Qi Physics — System Overview

The Qi engine transforms raw Four Pillars (BaZi) data into a functional,
month-by-month metaphysical model. This page documents the full pipeline.

---

## 1. TFQ (Natal Functional Qi)

- Derived from stems, branches, hidden elements
- Adjusted by birth season
- Weighted by DM/DB/Month/Year/Hour
- Produces the baseline Qi distribution

Each pillar contributes:
- **Stem**: 1 point to its element
- **Branch**: 10 points distributed among hidden stems by percentage

---

## 2. Seasonal Qi

Shows how natal Qi manifests **in the current month's season**.

Birth season multipliers amplify or dampen each element:
- Elements **in season** get higher multipliers
- Elements **out of season** get lower multipliers

---

## 3. Year Qi + Month Qi

Adds the environmental Qi of the year pillar (20 pts) and month pillar (10 pts).
These are the "incoming" influences that shift the natal balance.

---

## 4. Combined Qi → Clash → Control Cycle

Applies:
- **Harms** — branch-branch destructive interactions
- **Clashes** — opposing branch conflicts
- **Punishments** — three-way branch penalties
- **Control cycle pressure** — element dominance creates downstream pressure

---

## 5. TotalQi (Monthly Final Functional Qi)

This is the **real** Qi used for remedies. It represents how the chart
actually functions in a given month, after all environmental and
interaction effects.

---

## 6. Collapse Mode Detection

Structural collapse occurs when element distribution becomes extreme:

- **Single-dominant** — one element exceeds 2x the next strongest
- **Bi-polar** — two elements dominate equally, creating oscillation
- **Drained** — one element has nearly vanished
- **Inverted** — Day Master element is weakest in own chart

---

## 7. Yong Shen Selection

Chooses the **functional stabilizer** for each month:

- Normal charts: strengthen weak elements, exhaust excess
- Collapse charts: **Follow the Strong** — support the dominant element,
  exhaust through its child (Sheng cycle)
- Never use the controlling element in collapse structures

---

## 8. Bracelet Qi Units

Each bead contributes Qi based on:

```
QiUnit = sizeFactor × effectiveness × roleFactor
```

- Size factors: 6mm = 0.8, 8mm = 1.0, 10mm = 1.3
- Effectiveness: stone-specific resonance with its element
- Role factor: Yong Shen stones get 1.5x, forbidden stones get 0x

---

## 9. BRQ_A + BRQ_B (Bracelet Qi Physics)

Two-channel correction system:

- **BRQ_A** (reactive): 35% of the gap between TFQ and TotalQi — corrects monthly drift
- **BRQ_B** (proactive): 10% of natal TFQ — reinforces structural identity

Hard cap: ±15% of TFQ per element prevents over-correction.

Month-type tuning:
- Drained months: +25% leverage (more room to correct)
- Overcrowded months: -25% leverage (less room to correct)
- Normal months: 1.0x baseline

---

## 10. Radar Shift

Visualizes how the bracelet nudges each month toward stability:
- Faint bar = natal TFQ baseline
- Medium bar = current month TotalQi
- Bright bar = after bracelet correction
- Delta values show exact shift per element
