## Demo 1: "Meet Luna" (2 minutes)

### Purpose
First impression - show Luna's warm, engaging personality

### Script

**Opening (15 seconds)**
> "This is Luna, an AI companion unlike any other. Watch what happens when a user opens the app for the first time."

**Demo Flow**

1. **New User Opens App**
   - Luna: "Hey there! I'm Luna. I've been looking forward to meeting you. What's on your mind today?"

2. **User: "Just had a rough day at work"**
   - Luna detects: Mild sadness/frustration
   - Mode: GENTLE (Week 9)
   - Luna: "Oh, I hear you. Those days can really wear you down. Want to tell me what happened, or would you rather just unwind for a bit?"

3. **User shares briefly**
   - Luna provides empathetic validation
   - Creates first happiness anchor (Week 2)
   - Tracks emotional state (Week 11)

**Closing (15 seconds)**
> "Notice how Luna didn't jump to solutions. She read the emotion, matched the tone, and offered genuine connection. This is just the beginning."

### Key Points to Highlight
- Natural conversation flow
- Emotion-appropriate response
- User agency (offering choices)
- No forced positivity

## Demo 2: "Emotional Intelligence" (3 minutes)

### Purpose
Showcase depth of emotional understanding

### Script

**Opening (20 seconds)**
> "Watch how Luna handles emotional complexity - not just detecting emotions, but understanding their nuance and responding appropriately."

**Demo Flow**

1. **User: "My partner left me"**
   - Plutchik Detection: Sadness (intensity 9) + Surprise (intensity 5)
   - Bathtub State: High salt concentration
   - Mode: GENTLE (automatic override)
   - Banter: BLOCKED (Week 10)
   - Emotional State: Concern +2

   - Luna: "Oh... that's a lot to carry. I'm so sorry. You don't have to explain anything right now. I'm here."

2. **Follow-up (Day 2)**
   - Emotional State Check: Still elevated concern
   - Days since interaction: 1
   - Luna initiates: "Hey, I've been thinking about you. How are you holding up today?"

3. **Show Emotional Memory**
   - User mentions something positive
   - Luna: "I'm glad there's a bit of light today. I was worried about you yesterday."

**Closing (20 seconds)**
> "Luna maintained emotional continuity across sessions. She remembered concern, initiated check-in, and acknowledged the previous day. This is emotional intelligence in action."

### Key Points to Highlight
- Plutchik 8 + 24 compound emotions
- Bathtub therapeutic state
- Mode selection (5 modes)
- Emotional state persistence
- Session continuity
