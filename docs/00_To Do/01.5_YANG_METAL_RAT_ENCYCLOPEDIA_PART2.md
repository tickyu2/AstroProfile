**Water governs:**
- Kidneys and adrenal system
- Urinary system
- Bones and bone marrow
- The deep nervous system, the brain's processing depth
- Reproductive essence (精 jīng — the foundational vitality)

### Luck Pillars and 庚子

In BaZi, the Luck Pillars (大運 Dà Yùn) represent ten-year periods during which a specific elemental energy influences the chart. For 庚子 people, the most significant Luck Pillar dynamics:

**Metal Luck Pillars (庚, 辛, 申, 酉):**
When Luck Pillar energy matches the Day Master's element, the constitution is strongly supported. These are typically periods of greatest personal effectiveness — the sword at its sharpest. Yang Metal Day Masters often experience these decades as the most productive and consequential of their lives: their capacity to act decisively, implement their vision, and cut through obstacles is maximally supported by the seasonal energy of the decade.

**Water Luck Pillars (壬, 癸, 子, 亥):**
The Hurting Officer energy in the Day Branch is amplified by Water Luck Pillars. **Wood Luck Pillars (甲, 乙, 寅, 卯):**
Wood is the Wealth element for Metal. Wood Luck Pillars bring the Wealth domain into focus — material resources, practical achievement, and the exercise of Metal's capacity to control and direct Wood's generative energy. **Earth Luck Pillars (戊, 己, 辰, 丑, 戌, 未):**

**Fire Luck Pillars (丙, 丁, 巳, 午):**

For 庚子 people with weaker charts, Fire decades can be periods of external pressure, constraint, and the challenge of operating under authority that the Hurting Officer cannot easily accept.
