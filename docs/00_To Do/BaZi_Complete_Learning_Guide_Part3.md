## 4.7 庚 Yang Metal (Gēng): The Tempered Sword ⚔️

**Element:** Metal  
**Polarity:** Yang (Masculine, Active, Expressive)

**Core Essence:** Decisive precision, sharp clarity, refined strength, cutting through complexity

**What:** Yang Metal is the energy of **decisive precision and refined strength**. It's the person who cuts through complexity with clarity, who makes tough decisions without hesitation, who refines through elimination of everything unnecessary.

**Why:** Life requires **decisive action and clear boundaries**. Yang Metal provides the sharp clarity to cut through confusion, the strength to make hard choices, the precision to execute perfectly.

**When:** Yang Metal shines in **crisis decision-making, situations requiring clarity, surgery (literal or metaphorical)**, any role demanding precision and strength.

**Where:** In executive leadership, surgery, law, military command, engineering - any field requiring decisive precision and the courage to cut.

**Who:** Yang Metal people are the **decisive leaders, surgeons, warriors** who cut through to truth, who make the hard calls, who eliminate the unnecessary with precision.
