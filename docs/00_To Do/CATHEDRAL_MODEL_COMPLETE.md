## SEASONAL LEADERSHIP ROTATION

### How Many Pods Work at Once?

**Peak Season Pattern:**
- **1 pod leads** (home season)
- **1 pod supports** (next season's pod)
- **2 pods rest/observe** (opposite seasons)

**Example: Spring Leadership**

| Pod | Season | Role | Activity Level |
|-----|--------|------|---------------|
| **Spring Pod** | Spring | 🎯 **LEADING** | 100% effort |
| **Summer Pod** | Spring | 🤝 Supporting | 60% effort |
| **Autumn Pod** | Spring | 👁️ Observing | 20% effort |
| **Winter Pod** | Spring | 💤 Resting | 0% effort |

**This pattern prevents:**
- Burnout (no pod works >6 months at high intensity)
- Conflicting leadership (clear seasonal authority)
- Wasted energy (pods rest when not needed)
- Vision loss (Winter pod preserves long-term view)
