## 🔄 **5 ELEMENT THEORY**

### **The Five Elements**

```
        Wood (木)
          ↓
        Fire (火)
          ↓
       Earth (土)
          ↓
       Metal (金)
          ↓
       Water (水)
          ↓
      [back to Wood]
```

### **Generating Cycle (相生 Xiangsheng)**

**"What nourishes what"**

- Wood → Fire (Wood feeds Fire)
- Fire → Earth (Fire creates ash/Earth)
- Earth → Metal (Earth births ore/Metal)
- Metal → Water (Metal enriches Water)
- Water → Wood (Water nourishes Wood/growth)

**Example:**
```
You are Metal?
→ Earth generates Metal
→ Optimal partner has Earth element
→ Earth nourishes/supports Metal nature
```

### **Controlling Cycle (相克 Xiangke)**

**"What restricts what"** (avoid these pairings!)

- Wood → Earth (Wood depletes Earth)
- Earth → Water (Earth dams Water)
- Water → Fire (Water extinguishes Fire)
- Fire → Metal (Fire melts Metal)
- Metal → Wood (Metal cuts Wood)

### **Yin-Yang Balance**

**Heavenly Stems:**
```
Yang: 甲(Wood) 丙(Fire) 戊(Earth) 庚(Metal) 壬(Water)
Yin:  乙(Wood) 丁(Fire) 己(Earth) 辛(Metal) 癸(Water)
```

**Balance Principle:**
- Day Pillar: Opposite polarity (Yang ↔ Yin)
- Hour Pillar: Same polarity (harmony)

**Why?**
- Day = external, needs balancing force
- Hour = internal, needs harmonious support
