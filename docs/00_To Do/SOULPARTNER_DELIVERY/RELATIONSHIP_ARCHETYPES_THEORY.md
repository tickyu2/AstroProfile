## 🎯 **THE CORE INSIGHT**

**Not everyone wants the same relationship!**

Traditional compatibility systems assume:
- Everyone wants romance (5th house)
- Everyone wants partnership (7th house)
- Everyone values the same things

**But reality is:**
- Some prioritize friendship > passion
- Some prioritize intellect > emotion
- Some prioritize career > romance
- Some prioritize home > adventure

**DIFFERENT PRIORITIES = DIFFERENT OPTIMAL MATCHES!** 💎

## 💡 **HOW TO CHOOSE YOUR ARCHETYPE**

### **Ask Yourself:**

**1. What energizes you most in a relationship?**
```
Deep conversations → Intellectual Soulmate
Doing things together → Best Friends First
Physical intimacy → Passionate Lovers
Building something → Power Couple
Creating a home → Home & Family
Balanced all-around → Soulmate
```

**2. What do your past relationships tell you?**
```
Failed because: Too boring → Need Passionate or Intellectual
Failed because: Too intense → Need Best Friends or Soulmate
Failed because: No shared goals → Need Power Couple
Failed because: Different values → Need Home & Family
```

**3. What's your life stage?**
```
Young, exploring → Passionate or Intellectual
Ready to settle → Soulmate or Home & Family
Career building → Power Couple
Seeking companionship → Best Friends First
```

**4. What's non-negotiable?**
```
Must be my best friend → Best Friends First
Must stimulate my mind → Intellectual Soulmate
Must want kids → Home & Family
Must support my career → Power Couple
Must be passionate → Passionate Lovers
All of the above balanced → Soulmate
```
