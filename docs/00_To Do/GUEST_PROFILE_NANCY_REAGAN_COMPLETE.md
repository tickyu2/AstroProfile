## 💝 THE LOVE STORY: NANCY & RONNIE

### **How They Met (1949-1951):**

**Nancy's Story:**
- Struggling actress in Hollywood
- Name appeared on communist sympathizer list (mistake)
- Needed help from Screen Actors Guild president
- That president was Ronald Reagan
- She asked for dinner meeting to clear her name
- **She later admitted:** She'd seen him in movies, wanted to meet him

**The First Date (1949):**
- Ronnie was recently divorced (from Jane Wyman)
- Guarded, not looking for marriage
- Nancy was determined
- Her own words: *"From that first meeting, I knew he was everything I'd been looking for."*

**The Courtship (1949-1952):**
- Ronnie was cautious (burned by first marriage)
- Nancy was patient but persistent
- She made herself indispensable
- She supported his acting career
- She never pressured, but was always there
- **Her strategy:** Be what he needed

### **The Proposal (1952):**

**Ronnie finally proposed** - March 1952
- Nancy never doubted it would happen
- She had DECIDED he was hers
- And she made it so through unwavering presence

**Wedding:** March 4, 1952
- Small ceremony
- Actor William Holden was best man
- Nancy became Nancy Reagan
- **Her identity became:** "Ronnie's wife"

### **The Marriage (1952-2004):**

**52 years together**
- She called him: "Ronnie"
- He called her: "Mommy" (term of endearment, not Oedipal)
- He wrote her love letters constantly
- She protected him fiercely
- They were rarely apart

**Their Dynamic:**
- She was his shield
- He was her purpose
- She managed details
- He did big picture
- She protected him from threats (people, stress, overwork)
- He gave her complete devotion

**The Gaze:**
- Famous for looking at him adoringly
- "The Nancy gaze"
- Critics mocked it
- She didn't care
- **Her response:** *"I can't help it. When he walks in a room, I still get butterflies."*

### **The Love Letters:**

**Ronnie wrote constantly:**

> "Nancy, I'm sitting here trying to work but can only think of you. I love you so much. You're my life, my everything. Hurry home. Ronnie"

> "If Nancy Davis hadn't come along when she did, I would have lost my soul."

> "I more than love you, I'm not whole without you."

**Nancy's response:**
- Saved every letter
- Published them in 2000 ("I Love You, Ronnie")
- Her identity: being loved by Ronnie
- Her purpose: loving him back

## 🎬 BEFORE RONNIE: THE EARLY YEARS

### **Childhood (1921-1940):**

**Born:** Anne Frances Robbins
- Mother: Edith Luckett (actress)
- Father: Kenneth Robbins (car salesman, left when Nancy was baby)
- **Core wound:** Abandonment by father

**Raised by aunt and grandmother:**
- Mother toured with theater productions
- Nancy rarely saw her
- Lonely childhood
- **Developed:** Need for security, fear of being left

**Age 8:** Mother married Loyal Davis
- Neurosurgeon in Chicago
- Wealthy, stable, conservative
- Adopted Nancy (became Nancy Davis)
- **First security she'd ever known**
- Became proper, controlled, careful
- Learned: Appearances matter, control = safety

**Adolescence:**
- Girls' school in Chicago
- Debutante
- Smith College (graduated 1943)
- Wanted to be actress (like mother)
- But really wanted: husband, security, family

**Early Career:**
- Stage actress first (using mother's connections)
- Moved to Hollywood (1949)
- MGM contract
- 11 films total
- **She was:** Decent actress, not a star
- **She wanted:** To be a star
- **She became:** A wife instead

**Her Movies:**
- "The Next Voice You Hear" (1950)
- "Hellcats of the Navy" (1957) - with Ronnie
- Never a leading lady
- Always the supporting role
- **Metaphor for her life:** Supporting Ronnie

**When She Met Ronnie:**
- Ready to quit acting
- Wanted marriage, family
- Saw Ronnie as the answer
- **She chose:** Wife over actress
- Never regretted it

- Rushed to hospital

**The Trauma:**

- Her worst fear nearly realized
- Never felt safe again

**Her Response:**

- Restricted access to him

- Tried to predict safe days
- Kept it secret (mostly)
- **When revealed:** Massive scandal
- **Her response:** "I was trying to protect my husband. I'd do it again."
