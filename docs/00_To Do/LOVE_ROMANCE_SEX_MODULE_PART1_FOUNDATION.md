## 🔥 ELEMENT-BASED ROMANTIC PATTERNS

### FIRE SIGNS (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)
**72 zones total: 18 Fire zones**

**Love Pattern:**
- **Fall Speed:** FAST - instant chemistry, love at first sight believers
- **Love Trigger:** Physical attraction + shared adventure + admiration
- **Sustainability:** Burns bright initially, needs continuous excitement to maintain
- **Expression:** Passionate declarations, grand gestures, physical affection
- **Attachment Style:** Anxious-avoidant (fear of boring stability)

**Romance Pattern:**
- **Courtship:** Bold pursuit, dramatic dates, competitive wooing
- **Maintenance:** Spontaneous adventures, surprising, keeping things new
- **Evolution:** Needs escalation - each year must be bigger/better
- **Death:** Routine and predictability kill romance

**Sex Pattern:**
- **Driver:** Physical attraction + ego gratification + conquest energy
- **Pace:** FAST - high urgency, quick to arousal, frequent need
- **Meaning:** Self-expression, power dynamics, physical release
- **Frequency:** High (3-7x/week ideal when young, 2-4x/week mature)
- **Style:** Passionate, energetic, variety-seeking, dominant energy

**Gender Lens:**
- **Masculine Fire:** Aggressive pursuit, provider-protector, needs admiration
- **Feminine Fire:** Magnetic attraction, chooser-selector, demands excitement
- **Non-Binary Fire:** High intensity expression, leadership in intimacy

**Age Evolution:**
- **18-25:** Maximum impulsivity, serial relationships, learns through trial
- **26-35:** Channels passion into ambition, seeks worthy partner
- **36-50:** Passion balanced with loyalty, depth emerges
- **51-65:** Fire tempered to warmth, passion becomes presence
- **65+:** Joie de vivre continues, playfulness sustains
