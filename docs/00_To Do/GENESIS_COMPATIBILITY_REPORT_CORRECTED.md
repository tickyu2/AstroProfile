### Understanding the Cusp System

GENESIS uses a **3-type cusp system** based on the principle that zodiac signs don't switch abruptly - they blend at boundaries:

**1. BLEND-BACK Cusps (First 6 days of sign)**
- Primary sign energy: 70%
- Previous sign influence: 30%
- Example: Aries starts Mar 21, so Mar 21-26 = "Aries with Pisces Echo"

**2. PURE Signs (Middle days)**
- 100% single sign energy
- No blending with adjacent signs
- Example: Pure Aries = Mar 27-Apr 13

**3. BLEND-FORWARD Cusps (Last 6 days of sign)**

This creates **36 distinct cusp types** (12 pure signs + 24 blended cusps).
