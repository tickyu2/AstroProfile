```
║  CONTROLLING CYCLE (克 Ke - Regulating):                        ║
║  Water → Fire → Metal → Wood → Earth → Water                    ║
║                                                                  ║
║  Each element CONTROLS another:                                 ║
║  ├─ Water controls Fire (水克火 - water extinguishes fire)      ║
║  ├─ Fire controls Metal (火克金 - fire melts metal)             ║
║  ├─ Metal controls Wood (金克木 - axe cuts tree)                ║
║  ├─ Wood controls Earth (木克土 - roots break earth)            ║
║  └─ Earth controls Water (土克水 - dam contains water)          ║
║                                                                  ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```
