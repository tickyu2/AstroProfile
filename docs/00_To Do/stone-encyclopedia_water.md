## How to Read

- **Qi Bar** — Base effectiveness (0–1) before seasonal modifiers
- **Yang ☀** — Dense, heavy energy for extreme imbalances
- **Yin ☽** — Gentle, nurturing energy for mild deficits
- **Yin-Yang ☯** — Balanced, adaptable energy

> Final QiUnit = baseQi × seasonModifier × polarityFit. Seasonal strength follows the 旺相休囚死 cycle.
