## 🏠 THE 12 HOUSES SYSTEM

### **What Houses Represent**

```
HOUSES = LIFE DOMAINS (where planets act)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

While SIGNS show HOW you do things (personality)
HOUSES show WHERE you do things (life areas)

Example:
• Sun in Taurus (sign) = HOW you shine (steady, grounded)
• Sun in 10th House = WHERE you shine (career, public life)

Result: "You shine steadily in your career"

Your house strength = how many planets in each house
Strong house = major life focus
Empty house = less natural focus (can still develop)
```
