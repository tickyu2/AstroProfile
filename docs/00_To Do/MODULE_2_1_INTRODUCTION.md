## 🎯 Why Start With Sun?

**Because your Sun is your CONSCIOUS SELF.**

It's who you KNOW you are:
- Your willpower
- Your vitality
- Your basic personality
- Your ego structure
- Your sense of self

**Your Moon (Module 2.2) is your UNCONSCIOUS:**
- Your emotional needs
- Your automatic reactions
- Your comfort zone
- What you need to feel safe

**Your Rising (Module 2.3) is your INTERFACE:**
- How others see you
- Your social mask
- Your automatic behavior
- Your physical presence

**We start with what you KNOW (Sun), then add what you FEEL (Moon), then add what you SHOW (Rising).**

Baby steps. Khan Academy style. Building layer by layer.
