**Consciousness:** "I'm learning this energy"

- Fresh encounter with sign's energy
- High enthusiasm, developing skill
- Establishing patterns
- Identity forming within this sign
- Outward initiation

Even though Taurus is a FIXED sign, at 7° it's still INITIATING its fixed energy. The person is:
- Building material security (not yet established)
- Establishing values (not yet crystallized)
- Learning what stability means to them
- Still figuring out their Taurus expression

**Contrast with:** Taurus 17° (Zone 4, Core Meta-Phase) where security is ESTABLISHED and values are SOLID.
