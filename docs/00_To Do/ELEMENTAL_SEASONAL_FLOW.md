## 🔥 FIRE: THE ARC OF EXPRESSION

### The Journey: Spark → Radiance → Torch → Extinction

**SPRING (Aries) — The Spark:**
- **Role:** Initiator
- **Function:** Ignition of life
- **Energy:** First to move when opportunity appears
- **Metaphor:** The match strike, the seed breaking through frozen ground
- **Psychological State:** "I must act to exist"

**SUMMER (Leo) — The Radiance:**
- **Role:** Sustainer
- **Function:** Peak expression
- **Energy:** Keeps the fire burning when others tire
- **Metaphor:** The sun at noon, the hearth that warms all
- **Psychological State:** "I shine by being fully myself"

**AUTUMN (Sagittarius) — The Torch:**
- **Role:** Transformer
- **Function:** Carries wisdom forward
- **Energy:** Takes what was learned and spreads it
- **Metaphor:** The lantern on a journey, wildfire spreading knowledge
- **Psychological State:** "I expand by seeking truth"

**WINTER — No Fire Sign:**
- **Status:** 🔕 **FIRE DORMANT**
- **Why:** Winter has no warmth, no light, no oxygen for combustion
- **Function:** Fire must rest and regenerate
- **Psychological State:** "Action is dormant, vision takes over"

---

### 🔥 The Fire Ah-Ha Moment:

> **"Fire cannot survive Winter. It needs light, movement, and oxygen—all scarce in the cold."**

**What This Means:**

**For Fire Signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius):**
- Winter is your **challenge season**
- You may feel dimmed, frustrated, or restless
- This isn't failure—it's **elemental truth**
- Fire must rest before Spring's return

**For Winter-Born People (Capricorn, Aquarius, Pisces):**
- You may struggle with Fire's urgency
- High-energy Fire people may overwhelm you
- You thrive in quiet, structure, vision—not action

**The Teaching:**
- Fire signs: **Don't force ignition in Winter—rest, dream, prepare**
- Winter signs: **Honor Fire's need to slow down in your season**
- Everyone: **Respect the natural extinction and rebirth of action energy**
