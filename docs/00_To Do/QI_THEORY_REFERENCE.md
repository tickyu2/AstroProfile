## What Is Qi?

Qi (氣) is the fundamental life-force that circulates through all living systems. In Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), Qi is not a metaphor — it is the operational substrate of health, emotion, cognition, and vitality.

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Chinese** | 氣 (qì) |
| **Literal** | "Breath," "air," "vital energy" |
| **Nature** | Neither matter nor energy alone — it is functional activity |
| **Governs** | Movement, warmth, protection, transformation, containment |
