## TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

### How Co-Star Works:

**Data Sources:**
- NASA JPL (Jet Propulsion Laboratory) real-time planetary data
- User birth information (date, time, place)
- Astronomical ephemeris calculations

**Technology Stack:**
- AI/ML for text generation
- "Human-written snippets" mapped to planetary movements
- Algorithm that analyzes transits to user's natal chart
- Push notification system
- Social graph for friend connections

**House System:**
- Uses "Porphyry" (ancient but less accurate)
- NOT "Placidus" (standard used by professional astrologers)
- This makes ALL their calculations slightly inaccurate

**Notable Problems:**
- Houses sometimes calculated incorrectly (sign in multiple houses)
- Retrograde planets not properly interpreted
- Generic text snippets matched to specific transits

- **Expert feedback:** "Signs listed in multiple houses, which is not possible"

#### 5. **Generic AI Content**
- Text feels "slightly robotic"
- Repeats over time ("I've had a couple of repeats")
- Not truly "hyper-personalized"
- **User feedback:** "Daily reading is total garbage... WAY the hell off"

#### 6. **Expensive Premium with Limited Value**
- $6.99-$9.99 subscriptions
- Repeating content
- Unclear what premium features include
- **User feedback:** "Too expensive... considering I've had repeats"

#### 7. **Sex Content to Minors**

- No age verification

#### 8. **No Educational Component**
- Doesn't teach users to read their own chart
- No explanation of astrological concepts
- Black-box AI without transparency
- Users stay dependent on the app

#### 9. **Negative Mental Health Impact**
- Increases anxiety for prone users
- Discourages facing problems ("avoid them")
- No support or resources
- **User feedback:** "Scarily personal, making me wonder if Co has access to my texts"
