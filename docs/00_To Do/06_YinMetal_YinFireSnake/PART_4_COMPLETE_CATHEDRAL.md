## 10. ✅ Good Matches (70-84%)

### Overview

**Compatibility Range:** 70-84%
**Probability:** ~5% of population  
**Sustainability:** Long-term with conscious effort
**Energy Dynamic:** Generally supportive with friction points
**Conflict Style:** Resolvable through communication
**Growth Pattern:** Both grow through challenges

**What makes a match "Good":**
1. Base elemental support exists
2. Constitutional gaps partially filled
3. Requires intentional work (not effortless)
4. Conflicts are manageable (not destructive)
5. Both willing to bridge differences

**The difference from Excellent matches:**
- Excellent (85%+): Natural flow, minimal effort
- Good (70-84%): Rewarding, requires conscious effort
- Moderate (<70%): High effort, uncertain outcome

**Good matches are worth pursuing IF:**
- Both partners are emotionally mature
- Both committed to growth
- Constitutional awareness guides behavior
- Regular check-ins prevent drift
- Appreciation is actively maintained
