### The Philosophy: Stories > Checkboxes

```
❌ "Are you introverted or extraverted?"
❌ "Do you prefer logic or emotion?"
❌ "Are you organized or spontaneous?"
```

**GENESIS approach (WIN):**
```
✅ "Your flight is delayed 3 hours. What do you do?"
✅ "A friend cancels plans last minute. How do you respond?"
```

- Bypass social desirability bias
- Reveal ACTUAL behavior, not idealized self
- Cross-validate with constitutional data

## CORE QUESTIONNAIRES

### MODULE 1: PERSONALITY FOUNDATIONS

#### **Big Five (OCEAN) Example Questions**

**Openness to Experience:**
```
Scenario: "You have a free weekend with no plans."

Options:
A) Visit a museum or art gallery I've never been to [5 points - Very High]
B) Try that new exotic restaurant everyone's talking about [4 points - High]
C) Stay home and dive into a documentary series [3 points - Medium]
D) Meet friends at our usual spot [2 points - Low]
E) Catch up on household tasks and errands [1 point - Very Low]
```

**Conscientiousness:**
```
Scenario: "It's Sunday night. You have a big presentation on Friday."

Options:
A) Already finished - I completed it Thursday [5 - Very High]
B) I'll work on it Monday-Wednesday, review Thursday [4 - High]
C) I'll start Tuesday, finish by Thursday [3 - Medium]
D) I'll probably start Wednesday or Thursday [2 - Low]
E) I work best under pressure - Thursday night! [1 - Very Low]
```

**Extraversion:**
```
Scenario: "After a long, intense week of work, you have Friday evening free."

Options:
A) Call friends immediately - let's go out! [5 - Very High]
B) See if anyone wants to grab dinner [4 - High]
C) Maybe meet one close friend for coffee [3 - Medium]
D) Stay home but text friends [2 - Low]
E) Absolutely need alone time to recharge [1 - Very Low]
```

**Agreeableness:**
```
Scenario: "A team member presents an idea you think is fundamentally flawed."

Options:
A) Praise their effort, gently explore concerns [5 - Very High]
B) Acknowledge strengths, suggest improvements [4 - High]
C) Point out issues but offer to help fix them [3 - Medium]
D) Directly explain why it won't work [2 - Low]
E) Bluntly say this is a bad idea and why [1 - Very Low]
```

**Neuroticism:**
```
Scenario: "You sent an important email 2 hours ago and haven't heard back."

Options:
A) Panicking - did I offend them? Check email constantly [5 - Very High]
B) Worried - refreshing email more than usual [4 - High]
C) Slightly concerned but going about my day [3 - Medium]
D) Not worried - they'll respond when they can [2 - Low]
E) Didn't even notice - I'm busy [1 - Very Low]
```

**Total:** 10 questions per trait = 50 questions total  
**Scoring:** Average per trait, convert to 0-100 percentile

#### **MBTI Cognitive Functions Example Questions**

**Energy Direction (I vs E):**
```
"You just finished a major project. To recharge, you:"

A) Go out with friends to celebrate [E +2]
B) Call a friend to share the news [E +1]
C) Process internally, maybe journal [I +1]
D) Need complete solitude to decompress [I +2]
```

**Information Gathering (S vs N):**
```
"You're reading instructions to build furniture. You:"

A) Follow each step precisely in order [S +2]
B) Read through once, then follow steps [S +1]
C) Get the gist, improvise as needed [N +1]
D) Look at picture, figure out the concept [N +2]
```

**Decision Making (T vs F):**
```
"Your friend wants advice: take lower-paying dream job or keep stable income?"

A) Analyze: salary, benefits, career trajectory, data [T +2]
B) Consider pros/cons logically [T +1]
C) Explore how they feel about each option [F +1]
D) What does their heart say? What matters most? [F +2]
```

**Lifestyle (J vs P):**
```
"It's 8pm on a work night. Friend texts: 'Want to grab drinks now?'"

A) No - I have a routine and stick to it [J +2]
B) Probably not - I planned to rest tonight [J +1]
C) Maybe - depends on how I'm feeling [P +1]
D) Yes! Spontaneous plans are the best [P +2]
```

**Total:** 10 questions per dichotomy = 40 questions  
**Result:** MBTI type (e.g., ENTP) with confidence percentages
