The Three Fundamental Cycles
1. The Producing Cycle (生 - Sheng) - Creation & Nourishment
Water → Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → (back to Water)
This is the generative cycle - each element births and nourishes the next:

Water nourishes Wood (trees need water to grow)
Wood feeds Fire (wood burns to create flame)
Fire creates Earth (ash becomes soil)
Earth produces Metal (minerals form in the earth)
Metal enriches Water (minerals dissolve into water, metal condenses moisture)

In constitutional terms: Your "parent" element strengthens you. If you're Wood, Water people energize you. If you're Fire, Wood people activate your potential.

2. The Controlling Cycle (克 - Ke) - Regulation & Balance
Water → Fire → Metal → Wood → Earth → (back to Water)
This is the regulating cycle - each element controls/constrains another to prevent excess:

Water extinguishes Fire (obvious physical control)
Fire melts Metal (heat transforms metal)
Metal cuts Wood (axes chop trees)
Wood penetrates Earth (roots break through soil)
Earth dams Water (soil absorbs and contains water)

In constitutional terms: Your "controller" element keeps you in check. If you're Fire, Water people ground you. If you're Wood, Metal people provide necessary structure.

3. The Weakening Cycle (泄 - Xie) - Draining & Depletion
Water → Metal → Earth → Fire → Wood → (back to Water)
This is the exhausting cycle - each element drains the one that produces it (reverse of the producing cycle):

Water weakens Metal (metal gives its minerals to water)
Metal weakens Earth (earth exhausts itself producing metal)
Earth weakens Fire (fire burns itself out creating ash)
Fire weakens Wood (wood depletes itself feeding fire)
Wood weakens Water (trees absorb water to grow)

In constitutional terms: Your "child" element drains your energy. If you're Water, Wood people can exhaust you. If you're Wood, Fire people consume your resources.


What makes this mathematically perfect is that it's self-balancing:

No element is superior - each has power over one and vulnerability to another
Excess is impossible - controlling forces prevent any element from dominating
Depletion is prevented - producing forces continuously regenerate
Dynamic equilibrium - the system is always in motion, never static
