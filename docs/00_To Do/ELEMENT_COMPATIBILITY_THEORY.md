## 🔄 **THE 5 ELEMENT CYCLES**

### **Generating Cycle (Productive)**

```
Fire → Air → Water → Earth → Fire
 ↓      ↓       ↓        ↓       ↓
Air   Water  Earth    Fire    Air
```

**How It Works:**
- **Fire generates Air**: Fire creates hot air (convection)
- **Air generates Water**: Air movement creates rain/condensation
- **Water generates Earth**: Water nourishes soil, creates fertile earth
- **Earth generates Fire**: Earth contains fire (fuel, clay vessels)

**Natural Example:** Water cycle in nature

**Psychological Meaning:**
- One person's strength naturally feeds the other's needs
- Supportive, nourishing relationship
- Growth-oriented partnership

---

### **Controlling Cycle (Restrictive)**

```
Fire ↔ Water
 ↕      ↕
Air  ↔ Earth
```

**How It Works:**
- **Fire controls Water**: Fire evaporates water
- **Water controls Fire**: Water extinguishes fire
- **Earth controls Air**: Earth grounds and limits air
- **Air controls Earth**: Air erodes earth

**Natural Example:** Fire and water opposition

**Psychological Meaning:**
- One person's strength challenges the other
- Friction that requires work
- Growth through challenge
