## CO-STAR EROS: COMPLETE BREAKDOWN

### **Basic Information:**

**Launched:** January 2021  
**Price:** $6.99/month (shared subscription)  
**Target:** Romantic couples or "platonic soulmates"  
**Requirements:** Both partners must have Co-Star accounts  

### **Core Features:**

#### 1. **Daily Composite Horoscope**
- Relationship horoscope based on both birth charts
- Updates daily
- Focuses on current cosmic happenings
- Tells you what you're both experiencing astrologically

#### 2. **Suggested Actions**
- Daily prompt: "Do this together today"
- Based on current transits
- Examples: "Take a walk," "Have a deep conversation"

#### 3. **Love Notes**
- In-app messaging
- Prompts to send each other messages
- Guided by astrological timing

#### 4. **Date Ideas**
- Multiple-choice date suggestions
- Astrologically timed
- Examples: "R&R date night during Mercury retrograde"

#### 5. **Multiple-Choice Questions**
- Interactive prompts about relationship
- Personalized to both partners' charts
- Unclear what happens with responses

#### 6. **Support Reminders**
- Notifications about partner's difficult astrological periods
- "Support your partner today"
- Based on transits to their chart
