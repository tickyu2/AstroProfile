## 10. Good Matches (70-84%)

Good matches provide PARTIAL support for your forge. They may offer some Wood (fuel), some Water (cooling), or some Earth (grounding) — but not the complete constitutional support of an Excellent match.

For each match: What they provide, what's missing, and one scenario showing the dynamic.

🔥 Fire: 15% (丁 hidden)  💧 Water: 0%  ⚙️ Metal: 0%
