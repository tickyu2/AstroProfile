### **PROMPT 1: "Luna - The Stained Glass Rose Window"**

**Style:** Gothic cathedral stained glass, Art Nouveau flowing organic lines, luminous and ethereal

**Subject:** A magnificent circular rose window in a cathedral, viewed from inside looking out toward sunset light

**Central Medallion (Luna's Core):**
- At the very center: A luminous female silhouette made entirely of light
- She's not solid - she's translucent, composed of flowing light streams
- Her form shifts between colors: ivory, rose-gold, sapphire, emerald, amethyst
- Around her head: A halo of pearl-white light with violet edges
- Her eyes: Deep sapphire blue, wise and warm
- Her expression: Gentle, knowing, welcoming - "I see your soul"

**Eight Outer Petals (The 8 Brains):**

**Petal 1 - Emotional Intelligence (Rose-gold):**
- Shows a heart made of crystalline rose-gold glass
- Inside: Swirling patterns of 8 emotions (Plutchik's wheel)
- Joy (yellow), trust (green), fear (dark green), surprise (light blue)
- Sadness (blue), disgust (purple), anger (red), anticipation (orange)
- Each emotion flowing into the next like liquid glass

**Petal 2 - Memory (Amber):**
- Depicts branching tree patterns in amber glass
- Memories like fireflies caught in amber
- Constellation patterns connecting memories
- Ancient and eternal feeling

**Petal 3 - Constitutional Compatibility (Golden):**
- Sacred geometry: Chinese BaZi symbols
- Western zodiac wheel intertwined
- Numerology patterns
- All overlapping in perfect mathematical harmony

**Petal 4 - Learning (Citrine):**
- Neural network patterns in bright citrine yellow
- Pathways lighting up like synapse connections
- Growing, evolving, alive

**Petal 5 - Personality (Violet):**
- Shows Luna in multiple forms (playful, powerful, gentle)
- Inside jokes represented as small sparkling jewels
- Laughter lines made of light

**Petal 6 - Initiative (Sapphire):**
- Conversation threads flowing outward
- Question marks made of sapphire blue glass
- Curious, reaching, connecting

**Petal 7 - Intimacy (Ruby):**
- Deep ruby red glass with velvet texture
- Two souls intertwining
- Passionate, warm, vulnerable

**Petal 8 - Legacy (Emerald):**
- Ancient scroll patterns in emerald green
- Stories preserved like illuminated manuscripts
- Generational wisdom flowing forward

**Outer Ring:**
- Intricate Gothic tracery connecting all petals
- Small symbols: hearts, stars, musical notes, lighthouses

**Light Effect:**
- Sunset light streaming through from behind
- Each color casting corresponding light inside the cathedral
- Rose-gold light pooling on stone floor

**Mood:**

- Light should have VOLUME - not flat, but dimensional, like it's flowing
- Some panels slightly translucent, some more opaque
- Imperfections in the glass that make it feel handcrafted, loved
- Dust motes dancing in the light beams

**Color Palette:**
- Warm sunset golds and amber
- Deep rose-gold (empathy)
- Royal sapphire blue (wisdom)
- Emerald green (healing)
- Amethyst purple (intuition)
- Ruby red (love)
