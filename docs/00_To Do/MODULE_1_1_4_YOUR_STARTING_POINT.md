**In Module 1.1.1, you looked at the date ranges and thought:**
*"I was born on May 15, so I'm probably Taurus."*

**But here's the thing:**

The Sun doesn't read calendars.

The Sun doesn't change signs at exactly midnight on specific dates.

The Sun changes signs at DIFFERENT TIMES every year.

**Example:**

```
TAURUS-GEMINI CUSP (around May 20-21):

2023: Sun entered Gemini on May 21 at 3:09 AM UTC
2024: Sun entered Gemini on May 20 at 8:59 AM UTC  
2025: Sun entered Gemini on May 20 at 2:54 PM UTC

Same date range, DIFFERENT TIMES each year!

If you were born May 20, 2024 at 6:00 AM:
→ You're Taurus (Sun entered Gemini at 8:59 AM that day)

If you were born May 20, 2024 at 10:00 AM:
→ You're Gemini (Sun already entered Gemini at 8:59 AM)

Just 4 hours difference = DIFFERENT SIGN!
```

**This is why we need to CALCULATE, not guess.**
