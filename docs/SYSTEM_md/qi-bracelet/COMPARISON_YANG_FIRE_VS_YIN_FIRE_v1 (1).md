## CONSTITUTIONAL PHYSICS SUMMARY

Both are Fire. Both belong to summer. Both carry the same elemental mandate — **illumination** — and both are nourished by Wood and expressed through Earth. These shared foundations make Yang Fire and Yin Fire recognizably the same element even as they express that element at completely different scales and with completely different intentions.

| Shared Constitutional Foundation | 🔥 Yang Fire (丙火) | 🕯️ Yin Fire (丁火) |
|----------------------------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| **Element** | Fire | Fire |
| **Season** | Summer | Summer (evening hours) |
| **Organ pair** | Heart / Small Intestine | Heart / Small Intestine |
| **Shared mandate** | Illumination | Illumination |
| **Primary medicine** | Wood | Wood |
| **Primary expression** | Earth | Earth |
| **Scale** | Solar — warms everything simultaneously | Intimate — warms one thing completely |
| **Light axis** | Broad — the landscape | Precise — the face |

Both are driven by the Fire impulse to illuminate what is hidden and warm what is cold. What differs is **scale** and **intention**. Yang Fire burns for everything in its field simultaneously — it cannot choose who it warms. Yin Fire burns specifically — it illuminates what it has chosen to illuminate and withholds its warmth from what it has not chosen. Same mandate. Opposite radius.

## THE MYTHIC NARRATIVE

### 🔥 Yang Fire — The Sun (丙火)

Yang Fire is the solar blaze — the light that rises whether anyone is watching, the radiance that warms continents, the force that makes life possible across the entire visible landscape.

It burns because burning is its nature. It shines because shining is its truth.

Yang Fire does not choose its audience. It does not dim for comfort. It does not negotiate its brightness. It rises over the saint and the sinner with equal completeness, over the mountain and the valley without calibration.

Its gift is illumination — total, indiscriminate, transformative.
Its wound is the specific loneliness of the most visible thing in the world: everyone feels its warmth; almost no one asks about its weather.

> *Soul truth: "I rise because it is my nature. I warm because warmth is my gift. And I am allowed to be dim sometimes — without it meaning the sun is setting permanently."*

### 🕯️ Yin Fire — The Candle (丁火)

Yin Fire is the flame of intention — the light that appears only when conditions are right, the warmth that gathers people close, the glow that reveals what the sun's broad radiance obscures.

It burns because it chooses to. It shines where it is needed. It warms what it touches — specifically, completely, without wasting a single degree of heat on what it hasn't turned toward.

Yin Fire does not overwhelm. It does not blind. It does not scorch. But it can be blown out. And this vulnerability — the flame that requires shelter from the wind, that responds to every movement of air, that can be extinguished by a single careless breath — is not weakness. It is the source of its extraordinary intimacy.

The candle sees what the sun cannot see. It lights the face, not the landscape.

> *Soul truth: "I burn specifically. I illuminate what the sun cannot see. I am the warmth that finds you — not everyone, you."*
