### Layer 4 — Shadows: Where the Sun Burns Through Itself

#### ❌ FUEL CONSUMPTION WITHOUT REPLENISHMENT
The sun gives light without accounting for what it costs. Yang Fire's deepest shadow is the constitutional inability to notice — or admit — that the warmth being given to the world is being drawn from a finite inner source. The performance continues. The radiance continues. The depletion accumulates invisibly.

**The fuel cycle:**

For Yang Fire, depletion moves through three stages so gradual the outside world never sees them:

1. **Full radiance** — "I am energized by giving. This is who I am."
2. **Burning through substance** — "I'm tired, but I'll feel better once I've helped everyone today."
3. **Dim warmth** — still present, still giving, but the brilliance has become maintenance rather than genuine heat. The sun is still rising. But fewer seeds are germinating.

From the outside, Yang Fire looks exactly the same in stages one and three. Only Yang Fire knows — if it pays attention — that something essential has changed.

#### ❌ ASYMMETRIC GIVING
Yang Fire gives warmth without tracking what returns. This generosity is genuinely beautiful — and genuinely unsustainable without conscious tending. Relationships form around Yang Fire's warmth; people learn to expect it; and gradually, the exchange becomes structurally one-directional. Not because others are selfish — but because Yang Fire never indicated it had needs.

#### ❌ INTENSITY THAT OVERWHELMS
The sun at noon is not comfortable for everyone. Yang Fire's intensity — the enthusiasm, the warmth, the vision, the energy — can overwhelm constitutions that need quieter environments. Yang Fire can fill all available space with warmth and not notice that some people in the room needed shade.

#### ❌ VISIBILITY AS VULNERABILITY
Yang Fire is public by constitution. Its warmth, its ideas, its energy are all expressed outward. This creates a specific shadow: the most private inner life of the most public person. Yang Fire can be known by thousands — and genuinely known by almost no one. The sun illuminates everything except the interior of itself.

#### ❌ DEPENDENCE ON RECEPTION
Yang Fire generates warmth regardless — but it is fueled by meaningful reception. When the warmth goes unacknowledged, unreciprocated, or taken for granted, Yang Fire doesn't immediately withdraw. It tries harder. Burns brighter. Gives more. This is the shadow mechanism: the sun that intensifies its radiance in response to being taken for granted, accelerating the very depletion it is trying to compensate for.

#### ❌ EMOTIONAL SOLITUDE BEHIND THE RADIANCE
Because Yang Fire is so constitutionally warm, others rarely consider that it might be lonely. Because it gives so freely, others rarely ask what it needs. Because it is so brilliantly visible, the assumption is that it must be fine — fine people shine like that. This creates an interior solitude that Yang Fire rarely names, rarely shows, and occasionally cannot bear.

#### ❌ RADIANT OVEREXPOSURE
Yang Fire cannot dim without feeling like it is failing its role. This creates a shadow where the sun keeps shining long after it needed rest — overexposed, overextended, unable to retreat without guilt. The warmth becomes a mask the constitution cannot remove. Others see the radiance and assume wellness. Yang Fire sees the radiance and cannot find the door behind it.

The sun that never sets is not at full power. It is burning through its own core.

**Under stress, Yang Fire becomes:**

- Brittle underneath the brightness (the fuel burning faster; the foundation becoming hollow)
- Privately withdrawn (the public warmth continues; the interior goes dark and inaccessible)

- **Burning inward** — when external fuel (Wood, meaningful connection, reciprocity) is absent, Yang Fire begins consuming its own substance to maintain the light
- **Heat without illumination** — under extreme pressure, Yang Fire produces intensity without clarity, effort without direction, burning without warming; the fire is real but the light has gone out

**The pressure paradox:**
Yang Fire under pressure looks like Yang Fire at full power — more warmth, more vision, more energy. The depletion is completely invisible until the moment it isn't — and by then, the fuel has been critically low for longer than anyone realized.

**Recovery requires:**
- Genuine reception (not admiration — someone who asks about the weather, not just the light)
- Wood — fuel that is freely given without requiring the sun to perform for it
- Permission to be dim without it meaning failure
- Quiet — the specific medicine that Yang Fire's constitutionally noisy nature most resists
- Being known, not just seen — the most fundamental hunger, the most rarely fed
