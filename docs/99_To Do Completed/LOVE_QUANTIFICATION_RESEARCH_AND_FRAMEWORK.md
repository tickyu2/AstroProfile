## 2. SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH & CITATIONS

### 2.1 The Gottman-Murray Mathematical Model

**Researchers:** Dr. John Gottman (Psychologist) + Dr. James Murray (Mathematician)

**Method:**
- Measured couples' facial expressions, heart rates, skin conductivity, blood pressure
- Created mathematical equations to quantify positivity vs negativity
- Used differential equations (same math used to model war between nations)

**Key Findings:**
- **90% accuracy** predicting divorce from 15 minutes of conversation
- **5:1 ratio** - successful couples have 5 positive interactions for every negative
- Below this threshold, relationships mathematically spiral toward failure

**Insight:** The same equations model war and love - conflict spirals follow mathematical patterns.

**Source:** [The Mathematics of Love - Hannah Fry (The Marginalian)](https://www.themarginalian.org/2015/02/18/hannah-fry-the-mathematics-of-love/)

### 2.3 The 5 Love Languages (Chapman)

**Researcher:** Dr. Gary Chapman

**Framework:** How people give and receive love through 5 modalities:

| Language | Expression | Example |
|----------|------------|---------|
| **Words of Affirmation** | Verbal praise, compliments | "I love you", "You're amazing" |
| **Acts of Service** | Doing things for them | Cooking, helping, fixing |
| **Receiving Gifts** | Thoughtful tokens | Meaningful presents |
| **Quality Time** | Undivided attention | Deep conversation, presence |
| **Physical Touch** | Physical connection | Hugs, holding hands, intimacy |

**Key Insight:** People often **give love the way they want to receive it**, not the way their partner needs it.

**The Translation Problem:**
```
Person A gives: Words of Affirmation
Person B needs: Physical Touch

Result: A is loving, B doesn't feel loved
        → "Translation error" not lack of love
```

**Source:** Chapman, G. (1992). *The 5 Love Languages: The Secret to Love That Lasts*
