**How You View The World:**  
The world is **INFINITE HORIZON calling your name**. You see POSSIBILITIES EVERYWHERE. "Let's GO there!" Every moment is an adventure waiting to happen. Your HONESTY can be BRUTAL.

**How You Want To Receive Love:**  
**DON'T FENCE ME IN.** Love me without CAGING me. Come ON ADVENTURES with me. MATCH MY ENERGY - run with me, don't slow me down. Be DIRECT - I can't handle passive-aggressive games. CELEBRATE MY FREEDOM, don't resent it. And please: When I need to GALLOP ALONE for a while, TRUST I'm coming back. My freedom doesn't mean I don't love you.

**What Makes You Happy:**  
You're happy **IN MOTION** - traveling, running, dancing, anything that involves MOVING. You're happy when there's NEW HORIZON ahead - "We haven't been THERE yet!" You're happy in SPONTANEITY - unplanned adventure, sudden decisions. You're happy when you're FREE - no obligations, no cages, just OPEN SPACE. You're happy with PEOPLE WHO KEEP UP with your pace. You're happy when you're HONEST and people appreciate it. Your deepest happiness: "I am FREE. I am MOVING. I am ALIVE. No cage can hold me. The horizon is INFINITE and I GALLOP TOWARD IT with my heart WIDE OPEN."
